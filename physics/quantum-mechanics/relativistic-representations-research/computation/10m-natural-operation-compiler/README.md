# N10m computation — natural-operation compiler

Run from the repository root:

```powershell
node physics/quantum-mechanics/relativistic-representations-research/computation/10m-natural-operation-compiler/check-natural-operation-compiler.mjs
```

The compiler accepts a free power-carrier law, invariant resources, and an optional
coefficient-action certificate generated by N10w. It refuses a supplied token
grammar, relation table, or handcrafted Clifford law. From
the admitted laws it emits natural operations, typed degrees, rewrite relations,
construction provenance, omitted-operation obstructions, and bounded normal-form
certificates.

The check has three roles:

1. **Regression.** Exchange sign `+1` reconstructs the symmetric scalar grammar
   consumed by N10c. Adding N10w's generated metric-polarized coefficient action
   reconstructs the Clifford grammar consumed by N10c and N10g.
2. **Transfer.** Exchange sign `-1` constructs an exterior-power grammar. Its
   insertion and contraction obey an anticommutator, square to zero, and refuse the
   symmetric rank-two trace because its alternating projection vanishes.
3. **Use.** A separate bounded residual consumer receives the exterior grammar and
   constructs

   ```text
   R=P,  C=A,  D=Q-PA,
   DR=0,  D+RC=Q.
   ```

4. **Rank-boundary transfer.** A generic word is evaluated right-to-left on the
   nonnegative free-power tower. Operations that would cross below rank zero are
   removed with an obstruction trace. The same compiled bosonic packet therefore
   specializes to `Q` at spin zero, `Q-PA` at spin one, and
   `Q-PA+(1/2)PPT` at spin two. The Clifford packet specializes to `L` at spin
   one half and `L-PG` from spin three halves onward.

This is a first compiler cell, not a general invariant-theory engine. Arbitrary
Schur functors, multiple coefficient actions, critical-pair confluence beyond the
checked word bound, carrier selection, and physical cohomology remain explicit
boundaries.

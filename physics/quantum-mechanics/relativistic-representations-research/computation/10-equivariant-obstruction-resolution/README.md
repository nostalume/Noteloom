## Semantic question

For the fixed carrier and order budget

```text
G_s=H_(s-1),
F_s=H_s direct-sum U H_(s-2),
deg R=1,
deg D=2,
```

classify every Lorentz-equivariant pair `(R,D)` satisfying `D(p)R(p)=0`. Determine
whether the ordinary gradient plus Fronsdal symbol is the only nondominated branch
that has null-shell cohomology `C_(+s) direct-sum C_(-s)` and no off-shell
characteristic set.

## Exact composition table

For `epsilon in H_(s-1)`, introduce the four cubic channels

```text
q R_+ epsilon,
X epsilon=calH_s P^2 A epsilon,
U Y_0 epsilon=U q A epsilon,
U Y_1 epsilon=U calH_(s-2) P A^2 epsilon.
```

The projector construction and the operator relations give

```text
E_00^q R_+ = q R_+,              E_00^a R_+ = q R_+ + ((s-1)/s)X,
E_01 R_-   = X,

E_10 R_+   = U[((2s-1)/s)Y_0+((s-2)/s)Y_1],
E_11^q R_- = U Y_0,              E_11^a R_- = U Y_1.
```

Here is the reduction rather than an appeal to the finished table. From the trace
split of `P epsilon`,

```text
R_+ epsilon=P epsilon-(1/(2s))U A epsilon.
```

Using `A P=P A+q` and `A U=U A+2P` once gives

```text
A R_+ epsilon
 =q epsilon+((s-1)/s)P A epsilon-(1/(2s))U A^2 epsilon.
```

Multiplication by `P` followed by `calH_s` kills the explicit `U`-multiple and
turns the first term into `qR_+`; this proves the upper line of the table. Applying
`A` once more instead gives

```text
A^2 R_+ epsilon
 =((2s-1)/s)qA epsilon
  +((s-2)/s)P A^2 epsilon
  -(1/(2s))U A^3 epsilon.                         (C.1)
```

The one-trace decomposition at rank `s-2` computes

```text
calH_(s-2)P A^2 epsilon
 =P A^2 epsilon-(1/(2(s-2)))U A^3 epsilon.        (C.2)
```

Substitution of (C.2) into (C.1) proves the lower line. The calculation changes
only invariant operations and keeps the meanings “upper trace layer” and “lower
trace layer” visible at every step.

Every other block-mismatched composition vanishes. Hence the complete generic-spin
syzygy is

```text
r_+(d_00q+d_00a)=0,
r_+((s-1)/s)d_00a+r_-d_01=0,
r_+((2s-1)/s)d_10+r_-d_11q=0,
r_+((s-2)/s)d_10+r_-d_11a=0.
```

For `r_+r_- !=0`, the solution has two equation directions. This is already a
negative result: the full gauge identity does not uniquely select the ordinary
gradient or the Fronsdal branch. At `s=2`, the `Y_1/E_11^a` channel disappears;
`s=1` must be calculated in the one-layer carrier.

## Promoted classification

The invariant proof in N10 establishes:

1. every `rho!=0` is related to the ordinary-gradient ratio by the zero-order
   automorphism `S_rho=pi_0+(2s rho)pi_1`;
2. `E_s=pi_0D_s` has the same kernel as the full `D_s`, because the Bianchi
   residual `U A ell-2(s-1)P ell=0` and Fischer decomposition force `ell=0`;
3. the lower-target syzygy direction is redundant whenever the upper one is
   present and fails the physical dimension bound when it is alone;
4. the two singular gauge rays each leave at least `2s+1` unwanted classes.

Thus the fixed-budget realization has one supported local chain-equivalence class,
represented by the ordinary gradient, and one nondominated equation target
`E_s:F_s->H_s`. The lower equation layer is removed. Action completion is not part
of this verdict because changing the equation target need not preserve a
field-on-field formally self-adjoint operator.

## Executable regression

Run:

```text
node check-syzygy-branches.mjs
```

The script constructs harmonic trace levels and all invariant blocks, then checks:

- spins `2` through `6`, at three nonzero gauge ratios: zero cohomology at a
  non-null momentum and two classes at a null momentum;
- spins `2` through `4`: the upper equation alone has the same dimensions, while
  the lower equation alone has `2s+1` extra classes;
- a finite small-integer scan of both singular gauge strata finds no physical
  branch.

It uses floating Gaussian elimination with tolerance `1e-8`. It is an independent
finite-rank regression, not the proof and not an exhaustive numerical search. The
all-spin classification is carried by N10's automorphism, Bianchi/divisibility, and
dimension arguments.

## Carrier-changing compensator regression

[N10a](../../nodes/10a-unconstrained-compensator-resolution.md) constructs that fork
with an unconstrained rank-`s-1` parameter, an unconstrained rank-`s` field, and a
rank-`s-3` compensator. Run its independent check with

```text
node check-compensator-resolution.mjs
```

The script constructs the two gauge-invariant outputs directly:

```text
A_s=D_sphi-(1/2)P^3alpha,
B_s=T^2phi-4Aalpha-PTalpha,
```

checks `(A_s,B_s)(P epsilon,T epsilon)=0`, and computes the quotient at non-null
and null momenta for spins `3` through `7`. It returns respectively zero and two
cohomology classes for every tested spin.

Like the fixed-carrier script, it uses floating Gaussian elimination. N10a's
Fischer right-inverse and gauge-slice argument, not the numerical ranks, prove the
all-spin equivalence. The compensator bench is therefore closed for equation-level
realization; action and source response require the separate multiplier contract.

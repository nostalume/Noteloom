## Obstruction: the worktable has connected prose but disconnected objects

The current vertical paths cannot yet be composed unchanged:

```text
carrier grammar -> residual repair,
action ports -> graph/subset evaluation,
Hamiltonian departure -> visible return -> spectral cells.
```

The first path constructs operations but the latter paths re-encode them in
model-specific inputs. A graph list is not stable under Ward/contact repair or an
observable quotient. A scalar spectral measure loses coherent channel phases, and
a Schur complement does not generate the action whose contact terms it must retain.

Therefore the common object cannot be a carrier, graph, self-energy, measure, or
Hamiltonian alone. It must retain the smallest data needed to construct an
interaction, eliminate invisible internal structure, and recover a named
observable by more than one realization.

## Construct the typed problem datum

For a declared resource horizon `b`, form

```text
D=(X,epsilon,K,V,G,S,O,R,b).                              (40.1)
```

Its fields are operational rather than decorative:

- `X={X_c}` is the family of typed sectors or operator spaces;
- `epsilon_c` supplies Bose/Fermi grading and exchange signs;
- `K` is the free kinetic, Hamiltonian, transfer, or Liouville operation;
- `V=sum_r g^r V_r` is the supplied action/generator jet;
- `G` is an admitted inverse, homotopy, evolution, or boundary operation for
  internal composition;
- `S` is a prepared vector/channel interface or normalized state functional;
- `O` is the requested effect, correlator, channel map, or spectral response;
- `R` is the generated physical relation: equation, gauge image, constraint, or
  observable-null relation; and
- `b` bounds order, sectors, support, resolution, arithmetic, and work.

A domain adapter must construct these fields from its native presentation. It may
not place the expected observable value in `D`. Lagrangian, Hamiltonian, and
Liouvillian presentations are not identified: each adapter must certify the exact
native operation represented by `K,V,G,S,O`.

## Generate composition before selecting its realization

Let `F_b(D)` be the finite space of all well-typed composites constructible from
`V`, identities, pairings, and the external ports selected by `S,O` within `b`.
For each compatible internal port pair, `G` constructs a contraction. Their sum is
an operator `Gamma_G` that reduces the number of open internal ports by two.

At finite perturbative order the prequotient interaction is generated by

```text
x_D=Ext_(S,O) sum_n 1/n!
      [sum_k 1/k! Gamma_G^k] V^(tensor n).                 (40.2)
```

Type incompatibility makes a contraction zero; an unavailable Green domain or an
exceeded budget returns a refusal. Expanding the individual choices in (40.2)
produces graph histories, but those choices are not retained when they have the
same downstream action.

Exact feedback is admitted only when a domain supplies and certifies its fixed
point or inverse. If an internal propagation obeys

```text
R_Q=R_0+R_0 W R_Q,                                       (40.3)
```

then substitution gives

```text
R_Q=(1-R_0W)^(-1)R_0
   =sum_(n>=0)(R_0W)^nR_0                                (40.4)
```

where convergence or formal-order meaning is explicit. The left side is a direct
return realization; the right side recovers ordered excursions. An unnamed inverse
is not a compiler operation: the domain adapter must provide its solver, error, and
cost or stop at the finite series.

## Let physical and observable failure construct the quotient

Apply the physical obstruction supplied by `R` to (40.2):

```text

```

If this is nonzero, search only operations already generated by the same action

```text

```

This is node 19's contact construction in reusable form. If the available columns
do not span `-r_phys`, compilation refuses physical closure rather than inventing
a graph-specific repair. Call `D_R` a differential only when nilpotence is proved.

Let `x_cl` be the repaired representative. The observable descends only if

```text
I_phys subset ker Eval_O.                                (40.7)
```

Otherwise an externally generated compensator must repair the residual or the
request is refused. When (40.7) holds, construct

```text
a_D=[x_cl] in F_b(D)/I_phys.                              (40.8)
```

`a_D` is observable relative: another detector may see distinctions erased here.
The representative `x_cl` is retained only for graph recovery, provenance, and
regression; no inverse from `a_D` to individual graphs is promised.

## Realizations must meet at one target

A realization `rho_i` is admitted only with an evaluator and a common-target law

```text
Eval_i(rho_i(x_cl))=Eval_O(a_D)+e_i,   |e_i|<=epsilon_i.  (40.9)
```

The current domains instantiate different operations:

1. **Graph:** expand contraction choices in (40.2), preserving orbit weights and
   grading, then sum before applying `O`.
2. **Worldline/subset:** factor the same ordered histories through shared partial
   states, retaining a recovery map to the graph sum.
3. **Hamiltonian return:** for `P+Q=1`, eliminate `Q` to obtain

   ```text
   K_P(z)=PHP+PHQ(z-QHQ)^(-1)QHP.                        (40.10)
   ```

   Expanding the inverse by (40.4) recovers excursion histories; evaluating it
   directly gives the bound/open return when the boundary is admitted.
4. **Spectral:** in a self-adjoint branch, functional calculus turns the same
   departure into resolvent, time, and moment transforms. Positivity is not
   inherited by a general Liouvillian branch.
5. **Many-body/contour:** a state and boundary may turn internal elimination into
   an imaginary-time or Keldysh kernel. This remains a candidate realization until
   an unchanged post-freeze adapter passes.

This framework supersedes graph enumeration only when a direct or factorized
realization computes the same target with less complete work. It does not supersede
graph theory, nor claim that every internal inverse is tractable.

## Heterogeneous bench family

The existing material is classified before testing:

- nodes 19 and 27--31 are **design seeds and graph regressions**;
- nodes 32--37 are **return/measure regressions**;
- node 39 is a **same-action bridge seed**, because it helped shape (40.1)--(40.10)

The first independent transfer is a finite-bath single-impurity Anderson model:

```text
H_imp=epsilon_d sum_sigma n_(d,sigma)+U n_(d,up)n_(d,down),
H_bath=sum_(k,sigma) epsilon_k c_(k,sigma)^dagger c_(k,sigma),
H_hyb=sum_(k,sigma)[V_k c_(k,sigma)^dagger d_sigma
                    +conj(V_k)d_sigma^dagger c_(k,sigma)].       (40.11)
```

The adapter must generate its CAR grading and Fock signs, retain the local `U`
operation, and eliminate only the quadratic bath. Direct substitution of the bath
equation generates

```text
Delta_sigma(z)=sum_k |V_k|^2/(z-epsilon_k),               (40.12)
```

not as a fitted self-energy but as the bath return. The observable is one declared
impurity Green function, at zero or finite temperature fixed before execution.
The same `a_D` must support:

1. finite-bath exact diagonalization or a full resolvent as common target;
2. the direct hybridization-return realization using (40.12);
3. recovery of the bounded hybridization or interaction graph expansion; and
4. a complete cost comparison for the identical Green-function queries.

This transfer is discriminating because it changes bosonic to fermionic internal
statistics, relativistic/vacuum to condensed-matter interpretation, vector
preparation to an equilibrium state when finite temperature is chosen, and graph
enumeration to bath elimination without changing (40.1)--(40.9).

## Promotion, falsifiers, and horizon

Independent claim dimensions are action provenance, physical closure, observable
descent, graph recovery, cross-realization equality, cross-domain transfer, and
complete-route leverage. `C_response-v1` is rejected or split if:

- a backend needs information absent from `D` but essential to the common target;
- graph topology carries analytic/renormalization information that cannot be
  reconstructed from typed composition and boundary data;
- Hamiltonian and contour elimination do not preserve a common meaning of `a_D`;
- an adapter hides a domain-specific repair in the core;
- the physical quotient destroys a requested channel phase; or
- direct evaluation merely renames the expert baseline with equal or greater work.

The last item rejects leverage only. A failed cross-domain semantic mapping rejects
or splits the calculus itself.

The `v1` horizon is finite carriers or finite Fock sectors, UV/volume or bath
regulators, bounded perturbative order or certified finite inverses, one bound/open
Pauli--Fierz interface, one impurity Green function, and exact or error-bounded
common targets. It excludes continuum renormalization, thermodynamic and zero-
resolution limits, phase transitions, generic nonequilibrium Keldysh dynamics,
and universal Hamiltonian solution.

The Pauli--Fierz bridge, Anderson transfer, coherent information-loss case, and
their cost receipts are now classified. Applying `K-research-1` gives
[disposition `V_response-1`](../results/observable-interaction-v1-disposition.md):
the observable-relative semantic interface has bounded support, while a universal
scalar response and general complete-route leverage are rejected. No additional
QED graph, radial form factor, or impurity parameter scan may amend or promote this
candidate. Re-entry requires a reusable analytic-return reduction on two
heterogeneous consumers against their expert baselines.

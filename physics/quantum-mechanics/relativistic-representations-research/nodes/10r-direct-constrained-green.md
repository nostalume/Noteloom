## 1. The constrained equation supplies its own Euler form

For `h in H_s` and `epsilon in H_(s-1)`, the projected raise has the form

```text
R epsilon=P epsilon-U A epsilon/(2s).
```

The Fischer adjoints are `P^dagger=A` and `U^dagger=T`. Since `T h=0`, evaluate
the projected term on the same pair:

```text
<h,R epsilon>
  =<h,P epsilon>-<T h,A epsilon>/(2s)
  =<A h,epsilon>.                                      (1.1)
```

Thus `R^dagger=A` on the harmonic carriers. It follows by direct adjunction that

```text
D_s=Q-R_(s-1)A,
D_s^dagger=D_s.                                        (1.2)
```

The constrained gauge identity is not assumed. N10n generated

```text
A R_(s-1)=Q+[(s-1)/s]R_(s-2)A.
```

Apply `D_s` to `R_(s-1)epsilon` and use `A epsilon=0`:

```text
D_s R_(s-1)epsilon
 =-[(s-1)/s]R_(s-1)R_(s-2)A epsilon
 =0.                                                   (1.3)
```

Equations (1.1)--(1.3) construct the quadratic Euler/source interface directly;
the N10q section `S` does not appear.

## 2. Repeated raising constructs the invariant layers

The obstruction to inversion is the kernel in (1.3). To expose it without tensor
components, begin at every rank `l` with a transverse harmonic datum

```text
v_l in K_l=ker(A:H_l->H_(l-1))
```

and raise it to the field carrier:

```text
X_(s,l)(v_l)=R_(s-1)R_(s-2)...R_l v_l.                 (2.1)
```

Define `c_(s,l)` by the computed divergence

```text
A X_(s,l)=c_(s,l) Q X_(s-1,l).                         (2.2)
```

The generated commutation law acts on (2.1) and gives

```text
c_(l,l)=0,
c_(r+1,l)=1+[r/(r+1)]c_(r,l).                          (2.3)
```

Induction on the same recurrence produces

```text
c_(s,l)=[s(s+1)-l(l+1)]/(2s).                         (2.4)
```

At non-null momentum, surjectivity of `A` gives

```text
dim K_l=dim H_l-dim H_(l-1)=2l+1.
```

The sum of these layer dimensions is

```text
sum_(l=0)^s (2l+1)=(s+1)^2=dim H_s.                   (2.5)
```

The distinct values in (2.4), together with (2.5), show that the layers exhaust
the carrier. This is a semantic decomposition by the generated divergence action,
not a component expansion.

## 3. The Euler spectrum isolates one gauge layer

Substitute (2.2) into `D_s=Q-RA`:

```text
D_s X_(s,l)
  =[1-c_(s,l)]Q X_(s,l)
  =[l(l+1)-s(s-1)]/(2s) Q X_(s,l).                    (3.1)
```

The coefficient vanishes exactly at `l=s-1`. This is the gauge image generated by
`R_(s-1)K_(s-1)`. Every other layer is invertible away from the wave shell. Because
`D_s` is self-adjoint, its source annihilator is the orthogonal sum of precisely
those non-gauge layers.

Thus the representation of a generic physical source is already computed:

```text
source annihilator = direct sum of X_(s,l), l!=s-1.    (3.2)
```

## 4. Spectral interpolation generates the direct inverse

Normalize the rank-preserving operation by

```text
B_s=R_(s-1)A/Q.
```

Its eigenvalue on `X_(s,l)` is `c_(s,l)`. Instead of importing a propagator,
construct the polynomial `f_s` from the finite interpolation conditions

```text
f_s(c_(s,s-1))=0,
f_s(c_(s,l))=1/[1-c_(s,l)] for l!=s-1.                 (4.1)
```

The first condition removes the gauge layer; the others invert (3.1). The returned
quotient response is

```text
G_D=Q^(-1) f_s(B_s).                                   (4.2)
```

On every non-gauge layer, compute the composite on the same input:

```text
D_s G_D X_(s,l)
 =[1-c_(s,l)] f_s(c_(s,l)) X_(s,l)
 =X_(s,l).                                             (4.3)
```

On the gauge layer, `G_D` vanishes by (4.1). Equation (4.3) is the inverse
certificate; the interpolated formula is retained as the reusable operation.

## 5. Causal promotion and its theorem boundary

Each power `B_s^k` contains `Q^(-k)`. Together with the outer factor in (4.2), a
degree-`m` polynomial requires scalar-wave powers through `Q^(-(m+1))`.
On flat Minkowski space, the required boundary-selected retarded or advanced powers
are taken as a theorem contract. Constant-coefficient derivatives commute with
them, so (4.3) promotes to the corresponding causal quotient identity.

N10q already identifies the source quotients. If the direct and compensated routes
use the same retarded or advanced boundary condition, causal uniqueness on the
physical quotient makes their difference a gauge solution with the same support.
The N10j curvature annihilates that difference. Hence both routes recover the same
curvature observable.

The executable checks the rational symbol and invariant spectrum. It does not
reconstruct the distribution kernels or prove their wavefront bounds; those remain
the declared theorem boundary.

## 6. The generic cost comparison is negative

Exact interpolation gives:

| spin `s` | degree `f_s` | maximum Green depth | constrained component-Green load | compensated load |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2 | 2 | 3 | 27 | 10 |
| 3 | 3 | 4 | 64 | 20 |
| 4 | 4 | 5 | 125 | 34 |
| 5 | 5 | 6 | 216 | 52 |

The declared load is

```text
constrained: (s+1) times dim H_s = (s+1)^3,
compensated: one time [dim H_s+dim H_(s-2)] = 2s^2+2.   (6.1)
```

This metric counts sequential scalar-Green depth and carrier channels. It does not
pretend to settle parallel runtime, conditioning, sparsity, or a specialized
implementation. Within the requested generic compact-source capability, however,
the smaller harmonic carrier does not repay the layer separation: the compensated
route uses one scalar Green operation and is cheaper on both depth and (6.1).

## 7. Prepared sources expose the only immediate re-entry

If preparation supplies a source already known to lie in one layer `X_(s,l)`, no
spectral projector is needed. Equation (3.1) then gives

```text
G_D j_l=[1-c_(s,l)]^(-1)G_Q j_l,                       (7.1)
```

which uses one scalar Green operation. This is a real conditional advantage, not a
claim about generic sources. It changes the next question from “which carrier is
smaller?” to

```text
does a physical preparation naturally emit one invariant layer,
without first paying the generic layer-classification cost?             (7.2)
```

The generic projected-carrier source-response branch is now closed on its declared
metric. Re-entry requires a preparation satisfying (7.2), or a discretization where
the invariant projectors improve conditioning enough to outweigh their Green depth.
More carrier variants or spins cannot change this verdict.

## Research contract

- **Obstruction:** N10c--N10g generate equations and repairs only after receiving a
  token grammar. N10h explains two such grammars but its executable selects two
  finished packets. The operation algebra is therefore answer-bearing input.
- **Question:** can a carrier law generate the admitted natural operations and
  their relations strongly enough for an existing constructor and a new consumer
  to use them?
- **Independent input:** a free power functor on `V*`, its exchange sign, a
  nondegenerate invariant symmetric pairing, and optionally the coefficient-action
  certificate generated by N10w. No token list, relation table, expected field
  equation, or route name is admitted.
- **Benchmark:** regenerate both old symmetric packets; change the functor law to
  exterior power without adding expected relations; make a residual solver consume
  the result; retain exact refusals and provenance.
- **Horizon:** this node owns only the declared free-power cells and word-length
  three normal-form check. It does not select a physical carrier, prove global
  confluence, recover little-group cohomology, or claim a novel field theory.

## 1. Compile operations from what the carrier permits

Let `F_r(V*)` be the free power carrier whose multiplication has exchange sign

```text
epsilon=+1  for Sym^r(V*),
epsilon=-1  for Lambda^r(V*).
```

An invariant nondegenerate symmetric pairing `eta` converts the momentum covector
`p` to a contraction direction `p sharp`. The following operations are then not a
chosen vocabulary; they are evaluations of the supplied functor resources:

```text
P: multiply by p,                       rank shift +1,
A: derive/contract along p sharp,       rank shift -1,
Q: eta^(-1)(p,p),                       rank shift  0.
```

Projecting the symmetric metric into two carrier slots constructs `T` only when
its exchange symmetry matches the power functor. Thus `T` exists for `Sym` but its
projection into `Lambda^2` is zero. The compiler records this as an omitted
operation with an obstruction, rather than quietly retaining the symmetric token.

For the half-integer cell, N10w receives the first-order scalar-completion
capability and constructs an equivariant action `d(v)` whose polarized law is

```text
d(u)d(v)+d(v)d(u)=2 eta(u,v).
```

the same evaluations construct `L=d(p)` and the slot contraction `G`. No matrix
realization is used, and a handcrafted quadratic law is refused at the interface.
The current cell admits that action only on symmetric
powers; an exterior coefficient action is refused until its compatibility law is
independently constructed.

Exterior slot exchange is a carrier property, not Fermi statistics. The fermionic
local cell is `Sym^n(V*) tensor Delta` with the quadratic coefficient action; CAR
statistics enters only in the later free-quantization operation. This distinction
is now explicit in the manufactured manuscript.

## 2. Relations are evaluations of composites

Apply `A` after `P` to a carrier element `f`. The universal derivation can hit the
new momentum or pass through it with the carrier exchange sign:

```text
A(Pf)=Qf+epsilon P(Af).
```

Therefore the compiler emits

```text
AP -> epsilon PA+Q.                                  (2.1)
```

For symmetric powers, double metric contraction can hit either of the two new
slots, so

```text
TP -> PT+2A.                                         (2.2)
```

For exterior powers, repeated insertion and repeated contraction by the same
momentum vanish:

```text
PP -> 0,  AA -> 0.                                   (2.3)
```

For the coefficient-action cell, evaluating the slot derivation after coefficient
action and polarizing the quadratic law gives, among the other commuting rules,

```text
GP -> PG+L,
GL -> -LG+2A,
LL -> Q.                                             (2.4)
```

Equations (2.1)--(2.4) are the compiler trace: each rewrite records the common
carrier evaluation that produced it. They are not selected from the old scalar or
Dirac packet names.

## 3. The generated grammar must remain executable

The compiler checks that every relation stays inside the emitted grammar, that
each rewrite decreases a declared word order, and that normalizing every admitted
word of length at most three is idempotent. These are bounded termination and
stability certificates, not a claim of unrestricted Knuth--Bendix completion.

The symmetric output is passed without translation to N10c and reconstructs

```text
D=Q-PA+(1/2)PPT.
```

The coefficient-action output is passed to N10c and N10g and reconstructs

```text
S=L-PG
```

together with the retained Clifford source/Euler multiplier. This is regression:
it proves that the compiler has not weakened the previous constructive kernel.

## 4. A changed carrier law generates a changed system

Set `epsilon=-1` while leaving the invariant pairing and compiler unchanged. The
result is the exterior grammar

```text
{Q,P,A},
AP -> -PA+Q,
PP -> 0,
AA -> 0.
```

A separate residual consumer asks only for the roles “wave scalar,” “rank raise,”
and “rank contract.” It begins from `R=P`, enumerates the degree-two rank-preserving
span, and solves `DR=0`. The unique correction inside the admitted cell gives

```text
D=Q-PA.
```

It then solves `RC=Q-D` and obtains `C=A`. Direct normalization certifies

```text
DR=0,
D+RC=Q.                                              (4.1)
```

This is the decisive gain over N10h. The exterior relations and factorization were
not present in either handwritten packet, yet a downstream calculation uses them.
The result is recognizable as the differential-form gauge-wave symbol complex,
but that textbook name is a regression interpretation, not an input to the
construction.

## 5. Rank boundaries are generated rather than special-cased

A generic grammar still has to act on the nonnegative tower

```text
F_0, F_1, F_2, ... .
```

For a word `X_1 ... X_k`, composition acts from the right. The retained rank
specializer starts at the supplied input rank, applies each emitted rank shift
right-to-left, and removes a word exactly when an intermediate rank would become
negative. Its certificate records the first operation that leaves the tower.

Applying that operation to the generated bosonic residual gives

```text
rank 0: D_0=Q,
rank 1: C_1=A,             D_1=Q-PA,
rank 2: C_2=A-(1/2)PT,    D_2=Q-PA+(1/2)PPT.
```

No scalar or Maxwell equation is stored in the specializer. At rank zero, both
`A` and `T` would leave the tower; at rank one, `A` acts but `T` does not; at rank
two the trace channel first survives and forces the full repair.

The same operation applied to the Clifford residual gives

```text
tensor rank 0 / spin 1/2: S=L,
tensor rank 1 / spin 3/2: S=L-PG.
```

The first line follows because `G` would leave the tower. The second retains the
gamma-contraction obstruction and its generated parameter/field constraints.
Fresh executable checks certify all five reductions. These familiar names are
regression and transfer interpretations of rank-specialized output, not compiler
inputs.

## 6. What stronger generation now means

The supported reusable operation is

```text
NaturalOperations(
  free power law,
  invariant resources,
  optional generated coefficient-action certificate)
 -> admitted operations,
    computed rewrite relations,
    roles and adjoints,
    construction trace,
    omitted-operation obstructions,
    bounded normal-form certificates.
```

N10c is consequently demoted from the paper's conceptual front door to a
regression and residual-resolution backend. The stronger spine begins one level
earlier:

```text
metric + first-order scalar-completion capability
  -> generated coefficient action                       [N10w]
  -> carrier law + invariants
  -> natural-operation compiler                         [N10m]
  -> residual obligation and repair                     [N10c]
  -> field complex / source / action / response          [N10d--N10g]
  -> physical recovery and complete-route comparison.
```

This does not yet construct a carrier from a desired particle representation. It
does establish the missing middle tool: once a carrier is proposed, its local
calculus need not be rewritten by hand for every functor law.

## 7. Internal stop and next obstruction

The first N10m bench passes regression, transfer, and use. Repeating more named
free fields inside the same two power laws would now be verification work. The
next justified obstruction is **operation completeness**:

```text
Given a carrier expression and degree/rank bounds,
how do we enumerate all invariant natural operations from generators,
relations, dualities, and projectors—rather than recognizing only the
free-power operations currently programmed into the compiler?
```

[N10n](10n-projected-carrier-completeness.md) now performs the first such step on
the harmonic projector `H_r=ker T`. It generates the corrected raise from the
carrier-preservation obstruction, derives a rank-indexed rewrite law, independently
checks the same-family Hom multiplicities, and uses the law in a downstream
residual. The calculation also exposes a complementary mixed channel. Thus the
projected one-row cell is supported, while carrier-global generation is still open
at exactly that mixed-channel boundary.

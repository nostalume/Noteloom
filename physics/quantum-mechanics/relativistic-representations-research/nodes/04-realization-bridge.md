# Orbitwise realization bridge

Status: supported finite-orbit construction with explicit recovery obligations

## Capability and obstruction

Given a physical fiber and finite carrier, construct a covariant representative
without identifying the carrier with the particle Hilbert space.

Choose `B(p)k=p` and set `q=Lambda(A)^(-1)p`. The two transports to `p` are
constructed explicitly:

```text
B(p)k = p,
A B(q)k = A q = A Lambda(A)^(-1)p = p.
```

Their relative map therefore fixes `k`:

```text
W(A,p)=B(p)^(-1) A B(q),
W(A,p)k=B(p)^(-1)p=k.
```

This calculation constructs the Wigner element rather than importing “the little
group” as background.

## Intertwiner construction

At `k`, search for a stabilizer intertwiner `u_k:F -> V`:

```text
S(r)u_k = u_k rho(r),  r in K.
```

Transport it orbitwise:

```text
u(p)=S(B(p))u_k.
```

Then

```text
S(A)u(q)
 = S(A B(q))u_k
 = S(B(p))S(W(A,p))u_k
 = u(p)rho(W(A,p)).
```

Thus the covariance equation follows from a computed composite on the same fiber.

## When injection is impossible

For gauge potentials the physical fiber may be a quotient rather than a subspace.
At momentum `p`, construct a complex

```text
G_p --R(p)--> V --D(p)--> E,
             D(p)R(p)=0,
H_p = ker D(p) / im R(p).
```

A realization is supported only when an explicit map `H_p -> F_p` is bijective and
intertwining at standard momentum; covariance then transports the statement over
the orbit.

## Low-spin witnesses

- Massive spin one is recovered from the transverse kernel modulo no null gauge
  direction on the massive shell.
- Massless helicity one is recovered from the null transverse kernel modulo the
  line generated by momentum.
- Half-integer witnesses use abstract Clifford multiplication and its factorization
  law, not a chosen gamma-matrix table.

These are regression witnesses for the bridge, not its motivation.

## Output and edges

Output: orbitwise intertwiner or symbol-complex cohomology carrying the same
physical little-group representation.

- [Local symbol complex](05-local-symbol-complex.md) adds polynomial locality.
- [Equivalence boundary](08-realization-equivalence-boundary.md) distinguishes this
  recovery from causal, quantum, and predictive equivalence.

## Boundary

Standard-momentum cohomology does not alone prove global analytic domains,
source-space density, causal Green operators, action completion, or interactions.

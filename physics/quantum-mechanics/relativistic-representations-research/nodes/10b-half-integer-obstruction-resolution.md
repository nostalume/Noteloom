Status: supported for the constrained and compensator local equation complexes;
the first two gamma-Fischer equation layers are sufficient and layer-minimal,
while action/source equivalence and a carrier-global minimality theorem remain
separate  
Consumes: [N10 obstruction resolution](10-equivariant-obstruction-resolution.md),

Source contracts: [action-principle contracts](../sources/action-principle-contracts.md),

Produces: the obstruction-generated Fang--Fronsdal symbol and carrier constraints,

## Research contract

- **Defect:** N4b verifies a finished spinor-tensor complex. It does not yet show
  why its first-order symbol, gamma-traceless parameter, and triple-gamma-traceless
  field arise from failed cheaper attempts.
- **Question:** does N10's residual constructor survive the Clifford branch without
  choosing component gamma matrices, and which parts of the finished equation are
  actually required to realize helicity?
- **Invariant target:** for each tensor rank `n>=0`, the massless parity pair
  `C_(+(n+1/2)) direct-sum C_(-(n+1/2))`.
- **Declared search cell:** symmetric Dirac spinor-tensors, gradient gauge map,
  first-order natural symbols generated by `Slash_p` and `P_p Gamma`, followed by
  the smallest carrier enlargement generated by any residual.
- **Boundary:** this is a fixed-carrier/order obstruction bench. It does not claim
  that all Lorentz carriers or all natural first-order maps have been classified.

No matrix realization of the Clifford algebra is used in the derivation. N2b
constructs the Dirac carrier `Delta=S direct-sum bar S` and Clifford multiplication;
the present node uses only the resulting equivariant operator identities.

## 1. The gauge residual constructs the equation

On homogeneous spinor-tensor polynomials let

```text
P=P_p,       multiplication by the momentum linear form,
A=A_p,       contraction with momentum,
Slash=Slash_p, Clifford multiplication by momentum,
Gamma,       gamma trace,
Y,           gamma insertion.
```

The internally constructed algebra needed here is

```text
Slash^2=q,
[A,P]=q,
[Gamma,P]=Slash,
Gamma Slash+Slash Gamma=2A,
Gamma^2=T,                                      (1.1)
```

where `q=p^2`. Start with the cheapest first-order spinor equation, `Slash psi=0`,
and the cheapest potential redundancy, `delta psi=P epsilon`. Their composition is

```text
Slash P epsilon=P Slash epsilon,                (1.2)
```

so the equation is not gauge invariant. Inside the declared two-direction natural
ansatz, normalize the slash coefficient and write

```text
S_b=Slash+b P Gamma.                             (1.3)
```

Act on the same arbitrary parameter:

```text
S_bP epsilon
 =(1+b)P Slash epsilon+bP^2Gamma epsilon.        (1.4)
```

The two residuals have different gamma-trace degree. Cancelling the first forces
`b=-1`; the remaining obstruction is

```text
S=Slash-PGamma,
SP epsilon=-P^2Gamma epsilon.                   (1.5)
```

Thus the Fang--Fronsdal symbol and the gamma-traceless parameter are not two
external textbook choices. Within this search cell they are the unique correction
and the kernel of its uncancelled residual:

```text
G_n=ker Gamma subset Sym^(n-1)(M_C^*) tensor Delta. (1.6)
```

For `n=0` there is no gauge parameter and `S=Slash`.

## 2. Preservation constructs the field carrier

An equation-gauge composite is insufficient unless the gauge map also preserves
the declared field carrier. For `Gamma epsilon=0`, repeatedly calculate rather
than guess:

```text
Gamma(Pepsilon)=Slash epsilon,
Gamma^2(Pepsilon)=2Aepsilon,
Gamma^3(Pepsilon)=2A Gamma epsilon=0.            (2.1)
```

The first two expressions do not vanish for a generic admissible parameter. The
third does. Therefore the first gamma-trace kernel preserved by the gradient is

```text
F_n=ker Gamma^3 subset Sym^n(M_C^*) tensor Delta. (2.2)
```

Equations (1.5)--(2.2) construct the constrained complex

```text
G_n --P--> F_n --S--> F_n.                       (2.3)
```

The final target may be restricted to `F_n` because N4i computes the Bianchi
identity

```text
B S=(1/2)P^2Gamma^3,
B=A-(1/2)P Gamma^2-(1/2)Slash Gamma.             (2.4)
```

N4b's screen exact sequence then supplies the already proved recovery witness:
zero cohomology for `q!=0` and exactly the helicity pair on nonzero `q=0`.

## 3. The equation target contains one redundant gamma layer

The triple-gamma carrier has the constructive Dirac--Fischer split

```text
F_n=H_n direct-sum YH_(n-1) direct-sum Y^2H_(n-2),
H_r=ker Gamma.                                   (3.1)
```

Unavailable negative-rank layers are omitted. Let `pi_<2` retain the first two
layers. We prove

```text
ker(pi_<2 S)(p)=ker S(p)                         (3.2)
```

for every momentum. Only the reverse inclusion needs work. If
`pi_<2 Spsi=0`, write the discarded residual as

```text
Spsi=Y^2h,                  h in H_(n-2).         (3.3)
```

The Bianchi identity says `B(Y^2h)=0`. Using

```text
Gamma Y^2h=2Yh,
Gamma^2Y^2h=4n h,
[A,Y^2]=2P,
Slash Y+Y Slash=2P,                              (3.4)
```

this becomes

```text
Y(YAh+Slash h)=2n P h.                           (3.5)
```

Multiply by `Y` and use `Y^2=U`, the metric polynomial:

```text
U(YAh+Slash h)=2n P(Yh).                         (3.6)
```

For nonzero `p`, the scalar polynomials `U` and `P` are coprime. Componentwise
divisibility in the polynomial ring therefore gives `U | Yh`, so `Yh=Y^2g`.
Multiplication by `Y` is injective because `Y^2=U` and the polynomial ring has no
zero divisors; hence `h=Yg`. But the direct Fischer split has
`H_(n-2) intersection Y(Sym^(n-3) tensor Delta)=0`, so `h=0`. At `p=0`, both
equation symbols vanish and (3.2) is immediate.

The first two gamma layers therefore give the compressed equation target

```text
E_n=pi_<2 S:F_n->H_n direct-sum YH_(n-1).        (3.7)
```

Keeping only `H_n` cannot work for generic `n>=2`. In complex dimensions,

```text
dim F_n=6n^2+6n+4,
dim G_n=2n(n+1),
dim H_n=2(n+1)(n+2).                              (3.8)
```

Even if the head equation has maximal rank, its non-null cohomology has dimension
at least

```text
dim F_n-dim H_n-dim G_n=2n(n-1)>0.               (3.9)
```

Thus one layer is insufficient and two layers are sufficient. The fermionic
target is layer-minimal within the gamma-Fischer truncations. This differs from
N10's bosonic result: its Bianchi identity removes every lower trace layer, whereas
the Clifford equation intrinsically couples the gamma-traceless head to one
gamma-inserted layer.

## 4. Removing the parameter constraint constructs a compensator complex

Now let `epsilon` be an unconstrained rank-`n-1` spinor-tensor. The residual
`-P^2Gamma epsilon` in (1.5) constructs one rank-`n-2` compensator `xi`:

```text
delta xi=Gamma epsilon,
A_n(psi,xi)=Spsi+P^2xi.                           (4.1)
```

Then `delta A_n=0`. Removing the field constraint creates the second residual.
The operator identity

```text
Gamma^3P
 =P Gamma^3+Slash Gamma^2+2A Gamma               (4.2)
```

constructs the second invariant output

```text
C_n(psi,xi)
 =Gamma^3psi-(P Gamma^2+Slash Gamma+2A)xi.        (4.3)
```

Indeed, inserting `(delta psi,delta xi)=(Pepsilon,Gamma epsilon)` into (4.3)
cancels (4.2) term by term. The typed complex is therefore

```text
Sym^(n-1) tensor Delta
  --(P,Gamma)-->
(Sym^n direct-sum Sym^(n-2)) tensor Delta
  --(A_n,C_n)-->
(Sym^n direct-sum Sym^(n-3)) tensor Delta.        (4.4)
```

Negative ranks are absent. For `n<=1`, gamma-tracelessness of the parameter is
automatic and no compensator is needed.

## 5. Gamma-Fischer gauge fixing proves quotient equivalence

The relation `Gamma Y+Y Gamma=2r+4` on degree `r` makes gamma trace surjective.
More explicitly, for `h in H_ell`,

```text
Gamma(Y^m h)=c_m(ell)Y^(m-1)h,
c_(2j)=2j,
c_(2j+1)=2ell+2j+4.                              (5.1)
```

Every available coefficient is nonzero. Inverting it on each finite Fischer layer
constructs a right inverse `J_n` with `Gamma J_n=I`. Choose

```text
epsilon_xi=-J_n xi.                              (5.2)
```

Then `xi+Gamma epsilon_xi=0`; the transformations preserving this slice are
exactly `ker Gamma`. On `xi=0`, (4.1) and (4.3) reduce to

```text
Spsi=0,
Gamma^3psi=0.                                    (5.3)
```

Conversely every constrained solution embeds as `(psi,0)`, and two embedded
solutions related by (4.4) are related by a gamma-traceless parameter. Hence

```text
ker(A_n,C_n)/im(P,Gamma)
 ~= {psi in ker Gamma^3:Spsi=0}/P(ker Gamma).     (5.4)
```

The unconstrained system therefore has zero non-null cohomology and the same two
null-shell helicity classes as N4b.

## 6. Complete cost and capability verdict

Since `dim_C(Sym^r tensor Delta)=4 C(r+3,3)` and
`dim_C H_r=2(r+1)(r+2)`, for `n>=3`:

| Resource | compressed constrained realization | unconstrained compensator equations |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| gauge parameters | `2n(n+1)` | `4 C(n+2,3)` |
| field carrier | `6n^2+6n+4` | `4[C(n+3,3)+C(n+1,3)]` |
| equation target | `4(n+1)^2` | `4[C(n+3,3)+C(n,3)]` |
| maximum derivative order | `1` | `2` on `xi` in `A_n` |
| algebraic restrictions | `Gamma epsilon=0`, `Gamma^3psi=0` | none |
| recovery | direct screen quotient | construct `J_n`, set `xi=0`, then use the constrained quotient |

For free helicity realization, the unconstrained route is dominated: it replaces
quadratic-size gamma-irreducible carriers by cubic-size full symmetric carriers,
adds a field and equation, raises differential order, and reconstructs the
constrained slice during recovery. It purchases a different capability—an
unconstrained parameter and field interface.

A local unconstrained action is stronger again. The primary source in `AP-03`
requires a rank-`n-3` multiplier in addition to the rank-`n-2` compensator. The
present equation-only comparison neither charges nor claims that action/source
capability.

## 7. Cross-family verdict and stop condition

The same obstruction grammar now generates both parity-complete symmetric
families:

```text
cheapest kinetic symbol fails on a gradient
  -> its residual forces the correction and parameter kernel
  -> preservation forces the field carrier
  -> the Bianchi residual identifies removable equation layers
  -> relaxing the kernel constructs a compensator pair
  -> Fischer splitting proves recovery of the constrained quotient. (7.1)
```

The grammar is common, but its output is not uniform by analogy. Integer spin has
a second-order equation and one sufficient trace-free target layer. Half-integer
spin has a first-order Clifford equation and needs two gamma-Fischer target layers.
This is the desired discriminator: obstruction construction preserves semantic
structure while allowing the algebra to say where compression stops.

The cross-family regeneration bench is closed. Re-enter only for a complete
first-order natural-map classification, a carrier-changing competitor that can
beat this cost, or the stronger local-action/current-exchange capability. The next
spine question is no longer another familiar equation: it is whether the bounded
residual procedure itself admits a precise theorem and whether that theorem is
already contained in BRST, Spencer, BGG, or free-resolution machinery.

# Action-generated Hamiltonian departure

Status: `V_departure-1` supports finite Hamiltonian departure generation and
bound/off-axis composition; continuum and physical-packet transfer remain open

Consumes: node 18's return identity, `V_open-1`, `V_return-1`, and
`V_instrument-1`.

Produces: a pre-contraction departure generated from normal-ordered interaction
terms, plus a frozen test of one bound and one detector consumer on the same
Hamiltonian.

## Bind the weakest bridge

The visible instrument accepts `B:P->Q`, but no active compiler produces that map
from dynamics and preparation. Three nearby constructions each miss a different
part of the required composite:

1. node 19 retains an unsquared action-generated scalar--spin-two vertex, but node
   24 proves that its positive finite window does not descend through the external
   ideal;
2. node 29 generates a physical characteristic QED amplitude, but only after
   endpoint amputation, not as `QHP` in a common Hamiltonian;
3. node 32 has a common Hamiltonian and exact return, but supplies its scalar
   coupling function directly.

Calling any one of these `CompileDeparture` would hide one missing edge. The
minimal new object must retain the Hamiltonian interaction before squaring, know
how it acts on the preparation, and refuse an unresolved basis that drops a
generated channel.

## Construct departure from the interaction action

Let a finite bosonic occupation state be `|a;n_1,...,n_r>`, with matter label `a`.
The channel grammar is generated by

```text
a_k^dagger|...,n_k,...>=sqrt(n_k+1)|...,n_k+1,...>,
a_k       |...,n_k,...>=sqrt(n_k)  |...,n_k-1,...>.              (34.1)
```

Supply a normal-ordered forward interaction jet

```text
V_+=sum_j c_j |b_j><a_j| A_j,                  (34.2)
```

where each `A_j` is one declared creation or annihilation operation. Hermiticity
adds `V_+^dagger` to the opposite block; it does not erase the phase of `c_j`.
For a preparation isometry `J:C^p->H` and a declared unresolved basis defining
`Q`, apply (34.1) term by term and construct

```text
B=Q V_+ J,
B_(q,alpha)=sum_(j: V_j J e_alpha -> q)
             c_j sqrt(n_k+1 or n_k).            (34.3)
```

Equation (34.3) is not an input matrix: its entries are outputs of matter-label
matching, occupation change, Bose multiplicity, channel lookup, and aggregation.
If a nonzero generated state lies outside the declared `Q` basis, the truncation
cannot represent `Q V_+J` and must be refused.

## Make the two consumers share one Hamiltonian

Given admitted self-adjoint free blocks `H_P,H_Q`, the same generated departure
constructs

```text
H=((H_P,B^dagger),(B,H_Q)),
R_Q(z)=(z-H_Q)^(-1),
D_P(z)=z-H_P-Phi_B(R_Q(z)).                    (34.4)
```

Block elimination computes the bound spectral identity

```text
det(z-H)=det(z-H_Q) det D_P(z).                (34.5)
```

Thus below the channel threshold, zeros of `det D_P` are exactly the bound poles
of the same finite Hamiltonian, including multiplicity away from `spec(H_Q)`.

For a retained preparation vector `eta`, a channel effect `0<=F<=I`, and
`Im z!=0`, the unresolved block of the full resolvent is

```text
Q(z-H)^(-1)P=R_Q(z) B D_P(z)^(-1).             (34.6)
```

Consequently the finite-resolution detector response is generated without a
full-space state expansion:

```text
O_F(z;eta)
 =eta^dagger D_P(z)^(-dagger)
   Phi_B(R_Q(z)^dagger F R_Q(z))
   D_P(z)^(-1)eta
 =<Q(z-H)^(-1)P eta,F Q(z-H)^(-1)P eta>.       (34.7)
```

Equations (34.5) and (34.7) consume one generated `B` and one unchanged `Phi_B`.
They are a bound-pole generator and an off-axis channel detector, respectively.
The latter is not yet an asymptotic cross section.

## Frozen candidate `C_departure-v1`

```text
CompileDeparture(preparation Fock states, unresolved Fock states,
                 normal-ordered matter--boson vertices, budget)
 -> B + applied-term provenance + Bose multiplicity certificate
    + declared-basis closure certificate + resource receipt
 | refusal(inconsistent occupations, invalid mode/action, duplicate basis,
           omitted generated channel, empty departure, or budget).
```

Independent dimensions are term-action correctness, aggregation and phase
preservation, Bose multiplicity, truncation refusal, composition with the unchanged
visible instrument, bound determinant recovery, off-axis detector recovery, and
complete cost.

The post-freeze composition bench must use at least two retained states, three
unresolved channels, interfering signed couplings, a nontrivial detector partition,
and free blocks not encoded by the compiler. It must compare (34.5) and (34.7) with
direct full-Hamiltonian evaluation. A structurally different occupied-boson bench
must exercise both creation and annihilation multiplicities without changing the
compiler. Malformed bases and insufficient budgets must be refused.

No bench may add a model-specific rule to the frozen term action. Direct matrix
agreement is correctness evidence, not computational leverage: a cost disposition
requires a fair structure-aware baseline and recovery of the same observable.

## Execute the frozen benches

For two vacuum preparations and three one-boson channels, five signed vertices
generate

```text
B=((1/2,1/5),(0,1/4),(1/3,-1/6)).              (34.8)
```

All five Bose factors are one; no matrix entry is supplied to the compiler. With
`H_P=diag(-2,-1)` and `H_Q=diag(0,1,2)`, direct and reduced characteristic
determinants agree identically. Their five poles are

```text
-2.14439732871493, -1.07180613823380,
 0.147838007772076, 1.03105247590335, 2.03731298327330.           (34.9)
```

The first two lie below the channel threshold and are the generated bound
energies. At `z=i`, preparation `eta=(1,1)^T`, and
`F=diag(1,0,0)`, both sides of (34.7) give

```text
O_F(i;eta)=2961141300/27086113357.              (34.10)
```

An independent occupied-sector bench applies creation and annihilation to
`|e;1,1>` and obtains

```text
B_occ=(sqrt(2)g_c,g_a)^T,                      (34.11)
```

so the unchanged operation transfers the Bose multiplicity rather than assuming
vacuum coefficients. Truncated channels, duplicate bases, invalid modes, and
insufficient resources are refused. The executable certificate is
`computation/tests/test_departure_compiler.py`; the domain verdict is owned by
`../results/return-compiler-dispositions.md`.

## Horizon and stop rule

The horizon is finite matter labels and finite bosonic occupation bases with
linear normal-ordered vertices. It excludes gauge constraints, fermionic signs,
continuum limits, multiparticle closure, renormalization, and asymptotic scattering.
The composition, occupied-sector transfer, and refusal benches determine the frozen
dimensions, so this branch stops. Re-enter field physics only through a continuum
adapter or a Hamiltonian action packet whose physical quotient is already admitted.

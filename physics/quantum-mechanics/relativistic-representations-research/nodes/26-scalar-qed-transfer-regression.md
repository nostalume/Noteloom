## Generate the interaction jet from local phase transport

Let a local phase change act by

```text
phi -> exp(i e alpha) phi,
A -> A+d alpha.                                   (26.1)
```

The ordinary derivative does not transform with `phi`; direct differentiation
produces the unwanted term `i e (d alpha) phi`. The corrected derivative

```text
D_A phi=(d-i e A)phi                              (26.2)
```

cancels it:

```text
D_(A+d alpha)[exp(i e alpha)phi]
 =exp(i e alpha)(d+i e d alpha-i e A-i e d alpha)phi
 =exp(i e alpha)D_A phi.                          (26.3)
```

The scalar kinetic pairing therefore supplies, rather than assumes, the
interaction jet. Evaluate it on the same fields:

```text
<D_A phi,D_A phi>-<d phi,d phi>
 =i e <A,phi^* d phi-(d phi^*)phi>
  +e^2 <A,A> phi^* phi.                           (26.4)
```

Polarizing once in `A` gives the cubic scalar current. Polarizing twice gives a
factor of two, so after extracting the common amplitude factor `i e^2` the local
four-point operation has the invariant fiber form

```text
C_c=c eta,       c_action=2.                      (26.5)
```

The calculation below independently asks whether the Ward obstruction forces the
same coefficient. Agreement is a construction witness; the action coefficient is
not inserted into the residual solve.

## Construct the two internal homotopies

Use physical momenta

```text
p+k=p'+k',
p^2=(p')^2=m^2,
k^2=(k')^2=0.                                    (26.6)
```

The two possible partial scalar outputs generated by attaching one of the two
photon inputs are

```text
r_s=p+k=p'+k',       r_u=p-k'=p'-k.               (26.7)
```

Applying the scalar kinetic symbol `E(r)=r^2-m^2` constructs their defects:

```text
D_s=E(r_s)=(p+k)^2-m^2,
D_u=E(r_u)=(p-k')^2-m^2.                          (26.8)
```

On the generic internal domain `D_s D_u !=0`, the contractions are `h_s=D_s^(-1)`
and `h_u=D_u^(-1)`. Polarization of the cubic current on the corresponding scalar
momenta gives the transfer kernel

```text
T_ex^(mu nu)
 =-(2p+k)^mu (2p'+k')^nu/D_s
  -(2p'-k)^mu (2p-k')^nu/D_u.                    (26.9)
```

The two terms are generated by the two admissible insertions into the same physical
input request. The minus sign is the relative scalar-propagator/vertex sign after
the common `i e^2` factor is removed. No `s`- or `u`-channel name participates in
the construction.

## Calculate the incoming Ward obstruction

Contract the first photon port with its own momentum `k`. The first numerator
calculates its adjacent kinetic defect:

```text
k dot (2p+k)
 =(p+k)^2-p^2
 =D_s.                                           (26.10)
```

For the other internal output, use `p'-k=p-k'`:

```text
k dot (2p'-k)
 =(p')^2-(p'-k)^2
 =-D_u.                                          (26.11)
```

Substitution into the same transfer kernel cancels both homotopies:

```text
k_mu T_ex^(mu nu)
 =-(2p'+k')^nu+(2p-k')^nu
 =-2k^nu,                                        (26.12)
```

where the last equality uses `p-p'=k'-k`. Thus an incoming pure-gauge photon does
not vanish on the exchange transfer. It returns the local residual `-2k`.

The candidate contact operation evaluates on the same replacement as

```text
k_mu (c eta^(mu nu))=c k^nu.                     (26.13)
```

Both expressions have the same vector target. Exact residual cancellation requires

```text
-2+c=0       ->       c=2.                       (26.14)
```

This is precisely the independently generated action coefficient in (26.5).

## Calculate the outgoing Ward obstruction

The second photon port supplies an independent check. Direct evaluation gives

```text
k' dot (2p'+k')=D_s,
k' dot (2p-k')=-D_u.                              (26.15)
```

Therefore

```text
k'_nu T_ex^(mu nu)=-2(k')^mu,
k'_nu (c eta^(mu nu))=c(k')^mu.                  (26.16)
```

The same solve `-2+c=0` cancels this residual. On the common two-channel basis
`(incoming Ward,outgoing Ward)`, the compiler calculation is

```text
(-2,-2)+c(1,1)=(0,0)
  -> c=2.                                        (26.17)
```

The completed physical transfer is consequently

```text
T_phys^(mu nu)=T_ex^(mu nu)+2 eta^(mu nu),
k_mu T_phys^(mu nu)=0,
k'_nu T_phys^(mu nu)=0.                           (26.18)
```

The executable certificate represents momenta only in their invariant three-vector
span with a Gram pairing. It checks (26.8), (26.10)--(26.17), and the exact residual
solve without expanding spacetime components.

## Semantic result

The calculation exposes the role of every operation:

```text
cubic current
  -> creates a partial scalar output;

kinetic defect D_s or D_u
  -> measures its failure to be a free physical scalar;

homotopy D^(-1)
  -> transports that defect to the next local operation;

Ward contraction
  -> applies D and collapses the homotopy;

quartic action jet
  -> cancels the collapsed local boundary;

external projection
  -> admits only the completed transverse transfer.            (26.19)
```

Off-shell propagation is therefore neither an observable state nor disposable
notation. It is a witness for composing local interactions before the physical
quotient. The contact operation records the coherence required when that witness
is collapsed.

This also sharpens node 19. Its spin-two contact packet is not an exceptional
gravitational repair; scalar QED already has the same structural pattern. The
portable object is the topology-changing rule

```text
Ward contraction of internal homotopy
  -> kinetic cancellation
  -> local descendant
  -> higher action jet cancels descendant.                       (26.20)
```

## Complete-route verdict

The regression succeeds semantically but fails as a computational-advantage bench:

```text
transfer route: 2 exchange composites + 1 contact operation;
fair textbook route: 2 exchange terms + 1 seagull term.
```

The compiler generates the contact coefficient from a reusable two-channel
residual and never expands a Lorentz tensor, but the four-point route has no smaller
term count or integral count. This is a regression certificate, not the paper's
computability result.

The result changes the next research choice. Another four-point gauge example would
only repeat the supported pattern. Computational discrimination begins when one
representation combines a growing family of insertion orders or graph cells. The
nearest rejoining probe is generalized scalar-QED multiphoton transfer:

```text
homotopy-transfer insertion trees
  vs worldline master formula
  vs ordinary diagram/orbit enumeration,
```

with identical on-shell Ward quotients and explicit construction/recovery cost.
Only after a quotient physical integrand is available should the tropical sampler
be tested on its remaining metric-graph cells.

## Construct the criterion

Let `q:V->V/I_ext`. A linear detector `O:V->Y` descends precisely when
`O=bar O circle q`. One direction follows from `q(i)=0` for `i in I_ext`.
Conversely, if `O(I_ext)=0`, then

```text
bar O([v])=O(v)
```

is well defined because `[v]=[v']` implies
`O(v)-O(v')=O(v-v')=0`. Therefore

```text
O descends to V/I_ext iff I_ext subset ker O.    (24.1)
```

This equation is the executable admission law.

# Visible spectral measure and certified window

Status: one field-derived measure supports a bounded bound/open comparison;
finite-coupling and long-time claims remain uncertified

## Obstruction

Solving the complete dressed complement is often unnecessary, but postulating a
convenient density merely moves the problem. The retained measure must be generated
from the actual dynamics and preparation, and every approximation must carry a
remainder for the same observable.

## Construct the common measure

Supply self-adjoint `H`, preparation isometry `J`, `P=JJ^dagger`, and `Q=1-P`.
The departure map is

```text
B=QHJ.
```

Let `E_Q` be the spectral measure of `QHQ` on the cyclic subspace generated by
`ran B`. Then

```text
M_B(Delta)=B^dagger E_Q(Delta)B,
<u,M_B(Delta)v>=<Bu,E_Q(Delta)Bv>.
```

The second equality constructs positivity and shows that the total mass is
`B^dagger B`. Several outputs are transforms of this same object:

```text
Sigma(z)=integral (z-lambda)^(-1)dM_B(lambda),
K(t)=integral exp(-it lambda)dM_B(lambda),
mu_n=integral lambda^n dM_B(lambda),
rho(E)=boundary density or pushforward where defined.
```

Their domains differ; success of one transform does not authorize another.

## Field-derived bench

A massive scalar positive shell, smearing profile, mobile two-level preparation,
and coupling construct a fixed-total-momentum fiber Hamiltonian. Vacuum departure
gives a radial one-boson measure. Its pushforward under

```text
E(r)=sqrt(r^2+mu^2)+r^2/(2M)
```

feeds both an order-`g^2` ground/effective-mass coefficient and an emitted-boson
event. Profile, masses, gap, and coupling remain supplied dynamics/apparatus, not
representation-theoretic consequences.

## Certify the bound route

For a gapped prepared level,

```text
E_g=E_0+g^2 Sigma(E_g,g).
```

Subtracting the leading term gives

```text
E_g-E_0-g^2 Sigma(E_0,0)
 =g^2[Sigma(E_g,g)-Sigma(E_0,0)].
```

A local resolvent/Lipschitz estimate on the bracket produces the higher-order
remainder. The measure supplies the coefficient; the operator estimate supplies
the certificate.

## Certify the open route

Duhamel expansion of the same preparation yields

```text
Q exp(-itH_g)J
 =-ig integral_0^t exp(-i(t-s)QH_0Q)B_1 exp(-isE_0)ds+R_2(t,g).
```

The squared leading vector is a finite-time event computed from the Fourier
transform of `M_B`. A norm bound on `R_2` defines the admitted `(g,t)` window;
outside it the result is `uncertified`, not a decay rate.

# Visible spectral measure

Status: supported abstractly and for one regulated scalar benchmark

## Capability

Compute several bound and open observables without solving the complete dressed
complement. The retained object must be generated from actual dynamics and
preparation rather than postulated as a density.

## Construction

Supply self-adjoint `H`, preparation isometry `J`, prepared projection `P=JJ^dagger`,
and `Q=1-P`. The departure map is

```text
B=QHJ.
```

Let `E_Q` be the spectral measure of `QHQ` on the cyclic subspace generated by
`ran B`. Define the positive operator-valued measure

```text
M_B(Delta)=B^dagger E_Q(Delta) B.
```

This is constructed, not guessed: for prepared vectors `u,v`,

```text
<u,M_B(Delta)v>
 = <Bu,E_Q(Delta)Bv>.
```

Positivity follows by setting `u=v`; total mass is `B^dagger B`.

## One object, several transforms

```text
Sigma(z) = integral (z-lambda)^(-1) dM_B(lambda),
K(t)     = integral exp(-it lambda) dM_B(lambda),
mu_n     = integral lambda^n dM_B(lambda),
rho(E)   = boundary density/pushforward where it exists.
```

Thus resolvent poles/residues, memory and threshold tails, moment approximants,
and continuum events are projections of the same measure. Their domains differ;
one transform cannot silently authorize another.

## Field-derived scalar bench

A massive scalar positive shell, declared smearing profile, mobile two-level
preparation, and coupling construct the exact total-momentum fiber Hamiltonian.
Vacuum departure produces a radial one-boson measure. Its pushforward under

```text
E(r)=sqrt(r^2+mu^2)+r^2/(2M)
```

feeds both an order-`g^2` ground/effective-mass coefficient and an emitted-boson
open-channel probability. The profile, masses, gap, and coupling remain supplied
dynamical/apparatus data; representation theory does not choose them.

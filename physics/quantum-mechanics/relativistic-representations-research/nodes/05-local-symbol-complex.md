# Local symbol complex

Status: supported for declared finite symmetric/exterior/Clifford families

## Obstruction

An orbitwise realization need not be local in spacetime. Locality requires
polynomial momentum symbols whose kernel or cohomology recovers the physical fiber.

## Construction

Supply a finite carrier `V`, gauge carrier `G`, equation carrier `E`, and invariant
resources. Search bounded homogeneous equivariant symbols

```text
R(p):G -> V,  D(p):V -> E,  C(p):E -> K
```

and compute the residuals

```text
r_1(p)=D(p)R(p),
r_2(p)=C(p)D(p).
```

Coefficients are accepted only when exact solving makes these polynomial residuals
zero in the declared domain. At a standard momentum `k`, compute

```text
H(k)=ker D(k)/im R(k)
```

and construct an explicit intertwiner `H(k) -> F`. Equivariance transports the
same quotient along the orbit.

## Semantic operations

For symmetric carriers, multiplication `P`, divergence `A`, trace `T`, metric
insertion `U`, and wave scalar `Q` are maps between typed ranks. For exterior
carriers, wedge and contraction replace symmetric multiplication/divergence. For
fermions, Clifford action `G` is admitted only with its factorization law

```text
G(p)^2 = Q(p) I.
```

These operations are generated by [the grammar](13-natural-operation-grammar.md),
then solved by [the residual compiler](14-residual-repair-compiler.md). Components
are used only as finite certificates of ranks or signs.

## Action boundary

`D R=0` constructs a field complex, not an action. An action additionally requires
a pairing, density/orientation, formal adjoint, boundary domain, and normalization.
The Euler operator must be generated by variation or explicitly supplied; it is
not inferred from representation recovery.

## Output and edges

Output: a finite local complex plus standard-momentum physical cohomology and
failure certificate.

- [Bosonic machine](06-bosonic-free-field-machine.md) constructs causal and CCR
  completion.
- [Fermionic machine](07-fermionic-free-field-machine.md) constructs the Clifford
  and CAR branch.

## Checks and boundary

Exact certificates cover polynomial identities, ranks, quotient dimension, and
little-group action. The claim excludes mixed symmetry, arbitrary real structures,
interacting consistency, and unbounded/countable carrier completion.

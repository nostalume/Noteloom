# Graph-independent interaction return

Status: discovery candidate `C_interaction-return-v0`; its graph-independent
primitive and node 39's bench design are seeds for node 40's frozen successor

Consumes: node 13's generated operation grammar, node 19's action-forced Ward
packet, node 24's observable descent, node 27's history compression, and node 18/32's
non-graph return calculus.

Produces: a candidate algebraic object for which Feynman graphs are an optional
expansion, not the framework's input or output ontology.

## Capability and tension

The desired framework must carry the existing algebra compiler into interacting
dynamics and compute bound, scattering, spectral, or temporal outputs. Generating
the usual graphs more systematically is insufficient: it preserves the distinction
between histories even when the action, physical quotient, or observable does not.

Three calculated failures force a different primitive:

1. node 19's cubic graph is not Ward closed; an action-generated contact operation
   closes only the packet;
2. node 27's ordered histories have different syntax but collapse to common subset
   states before evaluation; and
3. node 32's complete return series is evaluated by one resolvent without forming
   its repeated-excursion graphs.

Thus neither an isolated graph nor a graph list is invariant under the operations
already needed by the worktable.

## Construct composition before graph expansion

Let the algebra compiler supply typed field sectors `X_c`, grading, dual pairings,
free kinetic operations `K_c`, admitted Green operations `G_c`, and the local action
jet

```text
V(g)=sum_(r>=1) g^r V_r.                                  (38.1)
```

The required first object is only the linear space `F` of type-correct formal
composites of these supplied operations. No graph is named. For every pair of dual
ports, construct a contraction operator `Gamma_G` by inserting the corresponding
`G_c`; incompatible colors have no contraction. Graded exchange supplies the sign.

For `n` action insertions, repeated contraction is generated by

```text
Contr_G(V^tensor n)
 =sum_(k>=0) (1/k!) Gamma_G^k[V^tensor n].                 (38.2)
```

The factor `1/k!` removes the order in which the same `k` contractions were made.
Combining (38.2) with the action normalization gives

```text
I_G(V)=Port_out [sum_(n>=0) (1/n!) Contr_G(V^tensor n)] Port_in. (38.3)
```

Expanding the individual choices made by `Gamma_G` draws ordinary decorated
graphs. Their symmetry factors and fermionic signs are therefore coefficients of
(38.3), not separately supplied annotations. Indeed, if the supplied action
normalization defines the relabelling group `H` of one contraction pattern `h`, its
coefficient is calculated on the same orbit:

```text
|Orb_H(h)|/|H|=(|H|/|Stab_H(h)|)/|H|=1/|Stab_H(h)|.       (38.4)
```

Graded orbit terms carry their exchange character before this sum. Keeping (38.3)
unexpanded retains the algebraic composite that graph enumeration normally
destroys.

This construction is still formal until contraction, action arity, grading, and
Green domains are supplied. It must refuse a missing pairing, an incompatible port,
an undefined characteristic inverse, or an order beyond the resource bound.

## Let physical failure close the interaction object

The free complex and the chosen physical variation construct a linear obstruction
map `D` on composites: for example Ward contraction or equation-of-motion descent.
Call it a differential only when `D^2=0` is separately certified. Applying it to
the cheapest interaction object computes

```text
r=D I_G(V) mod I_ext.                                     (38.5)
```

If `r` is nonzero, search only the next supplied action-jet operations `W_i` and
solve

```text
D[I_G(V)+sum_i a_i W_i]=0 mod I_ext.                      (38.6)
```

Node 19 is the seed calculation: `(1,1)+a(2,2)=0` constructs `a=-1/2`.
Equation (38.6) generalizes its meaning without importing a contact graph. Failure
to span `-r` is a physical closure refusal, not a request to invent another diagram.

Now admit an observable `O`. It consumes the physical class only when

```text
I_ext subset ker O.                                      (38.7)
```

If `ker O` is stable under every composition needed downstream, quotient by it;
otherwise retain `O` as a terminal evaluation and refuse claims of an observable
algebra. This prevents the word "ideal" from hiding a missing closure proof.

The resulting object is provisionally

```text
A_(phys,O)=closed interaction composites / admitted physical equivalence,
a=q[I_G(V)].                                              (38.8)
```

It is constructed because graphs failed closure, compression, and cross-observable
reuse—not because an abstract algebra was desired in advance.

## Graphs and returns become realizations

Quotienting intentionally forgets distinctions between graphs, so graph recovery
cannot generally be a map from `a`. Retain `x=I_G(V)` only as a prequotient
representative for regression. The two routes form the common-target diagram

```text
x in F_closed -> Graph(x) -> Ev_graph(Graph(x))
       | q                         |
       v                           v
 a=q(x)       -> Eval_O(a) ------> Y.                         (38.9)
```

`Graph` is a recovery and diagnostic map on the representative. Different graph
sums may have the same image under `q`; no inverse is promised. Correctness
requires the common-target calculation

```text
Ev_graph(Graph(x))=Eval_O(q(x))                           (38.10)
```

at a declared order, boundary prescription, and error model.

The return calculus is a direct realization of (38.8). For
`L_Q=L_(Q,0)+gW` and `R_0=(z-L_(Q,0))^(-1)`, compute

```text
R_Q=(1-gR_0W)^(-1)R_0
   =sum_(n>=0) g^n(R_0W)^nR_0,
Sigma_P=CR_QB.                                            (38.11)
```

The right side recovers excursion graphs; the left evaluates their common
interaction-return class without materializing them. Boundary values, spectral
transforms, and time evolution are different consumers of this class, not new graph
types. Node 27's subset recurrence is similarly a factorization evaluator for
(38.2), not the definition of the object.

## Candidate interface and falsifiers

```text
InteractionReturn(field datum, action, preparation, observable,
                  analytic boundary, order/resource bound)
 -> closed class a in A_(phys,O)
    + direct bound/open/scattering/temporal realizations that are admitted
    + retained regression representative x and optional Graph(x)
    + equality/error/cost certificates
 | refusal(typing, Green domain, physical closure, observable descent,
           analytic prescription, or resource bound).
```

`C_interaction-return-v0` fails or must split if:

- graph topology carries analytic or renormalization data not reconstructible from
  typed composition and its boundary prescription;
- physical closure requires graph-specific rules not generated by the action;
- the bound and scattering consumers cannot share a class without retaining all
  histories;
- an observable quotient erases phase or channel information needed downstream; or
- direct evaluation is only shorter notation and hides equal or greater total work.

The last failure rejects computational leverage, not the algebraic construction.

## Decisive bridge and horizon

Nodes 19, 27, and 32 are motivating seeds from different models; taken together
they cannot establish a common framework. The first decisive bench must use one
UV- and volume-regulated matter--boson action with an action-generated cubic and
contact operation, one preparation, and a coherent channel observable. From one
compiled class it must recover:

1. a conventional graph expansion from the retained representative at the frozen
   perturbative order;
2. a bound pole or level shift from its quotient class through the direct return;
3. an open-channel amplitude or detector effect through a boundary realization;
4. physical-closure and observable-descent witnesses; and
5. complete construction, direct-evaluation, graph-recovery, and observable costs.

The exact finite Hamiltonian is the independent common target. Success would show
that graph and non-graph predictions are realizations of one generated algebraic
object. Failure would locate whether typing, closure, analytic boundary, or
observable information prevents the proposed unification.

The initial horizon is finite carrier, finite mode/volume regulator, bounded
interaction order, and exact or certified finite arithmetic. Continuum removal,
renormalized loops, infrared completion, and nonperturbative phase structure remain
outside `v0`; they are later transfer obligations, not assumptions hidden in the
first bench. Node 40 now freezes the wider observable-relative successor and its
heterogeneous bench family. This node remains the graph-ontology seed and cannot
independently establish transfer for that successor.

## Construct the scalar alias

Let `B:P->Q` be a departure and let `U:Q->Q` be unitary with

```text
U H_Q=H_Q U.                                               (42.1)
```

Construct the phase-rotated departure `B_U=U B`. For every bounded spectral
function `f`, direct substitution reaches the same retained-space target:

```text
B_U^dagger f(H_Q) B_U
 =B^dagger U^dagger f(H_Q) U B
 =B^dagger f(H_Q) U^dagger U B
 =B^dagger f(H_Q) B.                                      (42.2)
```

Thus no collection of scalar spectral returns can distinguish the orbit `B -> UB`.
This includes the inclusive measure, self-energy, and projected resolvent wherever
they are generated only from the spectral algebra of `H_Q`.

Now supply a coherent channel effect `F`. The visible outputs differ by the
calculated residual

```text
delta_F(B,U)
 =Phi_(B_U)(F)-Phi_B(F)
 =B^dagger(U^dagger F U-F)B.                              (42.3)
```

If (42.3) is nonzero, a scalar-only evaluator receives identical data from (42.2)
but is required to return two different values. No algorithm on that summary can
meet the common-target law (40.9); it must refuse rather than guess a channel
phase.

## Let the obstruction generate the sufficient algebra

For a requested finite effect family `F_1,...,F_m`, construct the smallest unital
star-algebra

```text
A_req=star-alg(H_Q,F_1,...,F_m).                           (42.4)
```

The retained response is not an extra tensor. It is node 33's already constructed
operation restricted to the required algebra:

```text
Phi_B|A_req : A_req -> End(P),
A |-> B^dagger A B.                                       (42.5)
```

Its safe equivalence relation is generated by equality of (42.5) for every
`A in A_req`. If only inclusive spectral outputs are requested, (42.4) may collapse
to the commutative spectral algebra. Adding a coherent detector refines the
equivalence because (42.3) becomes visible. A compiled class cannot be silently
reused for a later effect outside `A_req`; the new capability must be compiled from
the retained action/departure or refused.

This construction preserves compression: remove the orthogonal complement of

```text
Q_cyc=span{A Bp : A in A_req, p in P},                    (42.6)
```

but do not identify directions inside `Q_cyc` that (42.5) distinguishes. If an
open channel kernel `B R_P B^dagger` is requested, its channel-valued capability
must likewise be included before quotienting; a scalar response is not enough.

## Execute a structurally different finite bench

Take `P=C^2`, `Q=C^3`, a degenerate channel Hamiltonian `H_Q=E I`, and

```text
B_+=((1,0),(0,1),(1,1)),
U=diag(1,-1,1),
B_-=U B_+.                                                (42.7)
```

Equation (42.1) holds for every `E`. Direct multiplication gives the common
inclusive return

```text
B_+^dagger B_+=B_-^dagger B_-=((2,1),(1,2)).              (42.8)
```

For the three coordinate projectors `P_1,P_2,P_3`, both departures also give

```text
Phi(P_1)=((1,0),(0,0)),
Phi(P_2)=((0,0),(0,1)),
Phi(P_3)=((1,1),(1,1)).                                  (42.9)
```

Hence even the complete coordinate-channel partition aliases the two inputs. Let
`F_12` project onto `(1,1,0)^T/sqrt(2)`. The coherent evaluations are

```text
Phi_(B_+)(F_12)=1/2 ((1, 1),( 1,1)),
Phi_(B_-)(F_12)=1/2 ((1,-1),(-1,1)),                      (42.10)
```

so (42.3) is nonzero. With `R_P=I`, the open kernels also differ:

```text
B_+ B_+^dagger=((1,0, 1),(0,1, 1),(1, 1,2)),
B_- B_-^dagger=((1,0, 1),(0,1,-1),(1,-1,2)).             (42.11)
```

The observable algebra generated by the coordinate partition and `F_12` makes
both cyclic ranks equal to three. The bench intentionally offers no further cyclic
compression; it tests information preservation rather than cost advantage.

# Representation, Fields, and Computable Observables

Status: supported bounded spine; carrier-global generation and interacting control
remain open

## Research target

The worktable asks whether a physical representation request and finite resource
budget can generate reusable local operations, expose their obstructions, and
reduce a named observable without hiding construction and recovery cost.

```text
physical representation
  -> finite realization and local complex
  -> generated grammar and obstruction repair
  -> supplied dynamics, preparation, observable
  -> visible response/measure
  -> bound or open output with error and cost
```

Symmetry constrains admissible representations and operations. It does not select
the action, interaction, state, preparation, detector, or approximation regime.

## Supported frontier

For separate finite massive spin or massless helicity in the declared
four-dimensional complex setting, the graph constructs physical fibers, finite
covariant realizations, local complexes, and selected free bosonic/fermionic causal
and one-particle completions.

Within supplied symmetric, exterior, and Clifford carrier laws, the computation
workbench generates bounded operation grammars, solves exact residual/repair
systems, and returns refusals when resources or depth are insufficient. It then
factors curvature-visible response and constructs radial visible measures that feed
both bound and open transforms.

The mechanics/collective branches reject a universal solver. Computational leverage
is claimed only where the same observable is recovered with construction, solution,
recovery, and error costs exposed.

## Open boundaries

- carrier-global and mixed-symmetry generation;
- completed countable-spin topology and real/parity structures;
- native interacting higher-spin/fermionic sources;
- finite-coupling dressed poles, resonances, and full scattering;
- anisotropic/nonmonotone measure generation without supplied partitions;
- measured runtime/conditioning advantage on concrete discretizations;
- generic nonequilibrium closure and controlled kinetic reconstruction.

Re-enter a branch only when new evidence can change a named edge, observable,
failure boundary, or reusable interface.

## Node and computation contract

Every supported node exposes obstruction, admitted inputs, semantic computation,
constructed output/refusal, certificate, downstream consumer, and open boundary.
Heavy algebra/numerics lives in the computation workbench; nodes retain the
smallest decisive equality. One locked command runs every retained certificate.

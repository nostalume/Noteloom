# Representation, Fields, and Computable Observables

Status: `C_response-v1` has restricted support as a typed observable-relative
semantic calculus; universal scalar compression and general complete-route
computational superiority are rejected

## Research target

Can a physical representation request and finite resource budget generate reusable
local operations, expose their obstructions, and compute a named observable more
directly than the complete baseline route?

```text
physical representation
  -> carrier and local free complex
  -> generated operations and obstruction repair
  -> supplied action/generator, state/interface, observable, and boundary
  -> internal composition or elimination
  -> physical and observable quotient
  -> graph/direct/spectral/many-body realization
  -> common-target result with error and complete construction cost.
```

Symmetry constrains admissible representations and operations. It does not select
the action, interaction, state, preparation, detector, boundary condition, or
approximation.

## Supported frontier

- Separate finite massive-spin and massless-helicity sectors generate physical
  fibers, finite carriers, local complexes, and selected free CCR/CAR completions.
- Bounded symmetric, exterior, and Clifford grammars generate operations,
  residual repairs, source adapters, certificates, and typed refusals.
- A supplied dynamics/preparation/observable generates a visible measure supporting
  certified bound and finite-time open transforms where analytic estimates hold.
- An action-generated scalar--spin-two packet closes internally modulo external
  equations; its Green contraction lowers without component tensor expansion to
  one nonlocal bubble and one local tadpole.
- The bubble continuum touches the free shell, rejecting an isolated Fock residue
  but generating an exact finite-resolution threshold law.
- Scalar-QED physical transfer generates its contact from the Ward obstruction.
  At four points this gives no cost reduction; for four photons, subset transfer
  evaluates 66 histories through 16 shared states and 56 transitions.
- Generated endpoint amputation turns that transfer into a characteristic
  gauge-invariant tree-amplitude class without inverting external kernels. Including
  per-input Ward certification, its combinatorial route first beats explicit
  histories at five photons.
- A supplied nonnegative detector density turns the class into a positive prepared-
  event effect after scalar/photon shell, transversality, and Ward admission. A
  generated two-body shell density then yields a finite scalar-Compton aperture
  measure, conditional normalization, common measure identity, and quadrature
  error. This is not an absolute cross section or infrared-complete observable.
- A parity-doubled spin-`1/2` carrier transfers the same kinematics and aperture
  through abstract Clifford pairings. Projected Ward residuals and projector
  residuals vanish, and the effect has a generated square-positive form. Quotienting
  before the trace leaves six blade coefficients and lowers effect-plus-Ward work
  from `1386` recurrence updates to `293` products; isolated full compilation falls from
  `11.321 s` to `0.981 s` while preserving the same aperture.
- A noncommutative subset recurrence now consumes the same exterior state. At five
  photons it recovers every ordered line history with `3317` products instead of
  `18292`; its endpoint Ward residual vanishes for every label in the retained
  four-photon fixture.
- A shell-generated three-photon nonlinear-Compton slice now consumes that state.
  The same positive finite-resolution measure is recovered by both routes; including
  three projected Ward checks and the effect costs `801` products instead of `980`.
  The soft poles are explicit, so this is not an infrared-inclusive cross section.
- Each of its two soft endpoints factors exactly into the unquotiented hard Compton
  density, the photon shell measure, and an eikonal current square. One positive
  kernel now generates virtual, unresolved, and resolved leading-soft bin weights;
  interval additivity proves their sum and first-order channel residuals vanish.
  Exact Laurent extraction also finds a nonzero `-1/E` term on each arm, so the
  compiler refuses the stronger claim of complete infrared cancellation.
- Re-entering that branch through exterior response jets separates the subleading
  density coefficient into a `+a` shell-phase tangent and a `-2a` amplitude
  interference tangent, giving the observed `-a/E` term with zero coefficient
  residual. This constructs the signed logarithmic obstruction without gamma
  components, but it currently differentiates an already compiled subset amplitude;
  recurrence-level complexity leverage and the virtual partner remain open.
- A scalar visible measure is now proved insufficient for coherent multichannel
  readout. Retaining `Phi_B(A)=B^dagger A B` instead generates inclusive and
  partitioned returns, coherent effects, an open kernel, and the exact visible
  cyclic sector on a post-freeze finite bench. This local result establishes
  information sufficiency, not an action compiler or cost advantage.
- A finite normal-ordered matter--boson interaction now generates `B=QVP` before
  contraction. The same `B` and unchanged `Phi_B` recover the full Hamiltonian's
  bound determinant and an off-axis channel detector exactly; an occupied-sector
  transfer generates the Bose square-root factors. This is semantic composition,
  not yet continuum physics or a cheaper route than expert Schur elimination.
- Exact Gaussian cell masses now turn that finite creation action into node 11's
  radial measure. One variation-plus-tail law contains both bound and finite-time
  open errors under refinement, but its first-order Lipschitz estimate is hundreds
  of times looser than the observed error and cannot yet select work efficiently.
- First energy moments now cancel the cellwise linear spectral residual and one
  second-moment law certifies both transforms with quadratic refinement. The
  retained summary wins only for repeated query batches after preprocessing; it is
  neither a uniformly better quadrature rule nor cross-form-factor evidence.
- The same shell/moment/action compiler now transfers unchanged from an analytic
  Gaussian mass adapter to a numerical algebraic-tail adapter. Both observable
  certificates survive with reported integration error, but complete cost rejects

- The graph, return, and measure paths motivated one frozen semantic candidate:

  contraction compression. Complete-route leverage is rejected because ordered-
  time integration makes the determinant route much slower on the frozen target.
- A post-freeze phase-orbit counterexample now rejects any universal scalar
  response object: identical inclusive and coordinate-channel returns can have
  different coherent effects and open kernels. Restricting the already frozen
  operator-valued map to the algebra generated by the requested observables
  preserves the phase exactly; this is information sufficiency, not cost leverage.
- The final same-action Pauli--Fierz bridge generates its linear and contact
  operations from one kinetic square, recovers all second-order return histories,
  and uses one return for a bound pole and coherent open response. It ties the
  expert Schur count, so semantic composition passes while computational leverage
  fails.
- [The correlation-functional return](nodes/43-correlation-functional-return.md)
  replaces transition coordinates in stages: cyclic projection preserves `2r`
  moments, the Gibbs state constructs the positive Liouvillian metric, 21 scalar
  moments recover the rank-ten response, and one endpoint recurrence reproduces
  the fourth-order Green coefficients within `5.63e-17`. The construction is exact,
  but its generic residual certificate is loose and its free Hankel form has rank
  two, so a fixed-rank Jacobi compiler is rejected.
- [The graded return port](nodes/47-graded-return-functional-port.md) resolves that
  singularity by coupling-valued reachability. Ranks `(2,6,12,22,30)` generate a
  1,985-scalar packet and recover three frequency families within `8.46e-16`;
  frequency reuse breaks even only after 70 queries. An independent closed-path
  evaluator verifies the same port within `1.11e-12` but is slower, so it supplies
  semantic evidence rather than leverage.
- [The native CAR return compiler](nodes/49-native-car-return.md) factorizes the 30
  insertions into 77 local--bath factors and 66 charge-homogeneous words. A post-hoc
  Wick/Pfaffian port is slower and less certifiable, while moving the words before
  closure generates the exact rank-30 module with 294 retained coefficients and
  128,000 counted operations instead of 122,880 matrix slots and 7,794,688 dense
  operations. Small-fixture runtime leverage remains unresolved.
- [The native thermal boundary](nodes/53-native-thermal-boundary.md) shows where the
  algebra compiler stops transferring. Gibbs right action escapes with residual
  `0.553`; direct closure crosses 101.9 million work units, and both the trace-Hankel
  and final-Green continuation spaces retain all 120 directions. The exact packet
  costs 407.4 million units and the five Green coefficients agree within `2e-8`, so
  invariant thermal compression and complete-route leverage are rejected under the
  frozen contract.

These statements establish reusable construction and bounded leverage, not a

general complete-route field/mechanics leverage is rejected for the tested routes.

1. Re-enter [the stopped native thermal branch](nodes/53-native-thermal-boundary.md)
   only with a changed semantic contract: a certified finite time/temperature or
   accuracy window, a different observable algebra, or a generated state primitive
   that can change semantic rank or complete-route cost. Do not optimize the same
   exact rank-120 realization.
2. Re-enter [the radial dispositions](results/return-compiler-dispositions.md) only
   for a named interacting consumer, a nonmonotone/multibranch or operator-valued
   channel, or interval-rigorous error demanded downstream. Another isotropic form
   factor cannot change the bounded-transfer verdict.
3. Continue [node 31](nodes/31-resolution-indexed-soft-channel.md) only through a
   recurrence-level Laurent lift. It must recover the two soft coefficients without
   first constructing the full rational amplitude and must report the complete
   transformation count. Only then construct a virtual operation on the same signed
   coefficient and detector bins.
4. Generate boundary/soft/asymptotic operations whose Ward images repair node 24’s
   scalar--spin-two external ideal on the characteristic set `q^2=0`.
5. Construct loop-level physical quotient cells only after the same observable and
   signed Ward cancellations survive direct, worldline, loop-tree, or tropical
   evaluation.
6. Reopen carrier-global or countable-spin construction only with a new generated
   operation, failure boundary, or reusable interface.
7. Reject any route whose discovery, construction, evaluation, recovery, and error
   cost is not lower than its fair baseline.

Do not continue a branch merely to accumulate examples, master integrals, or
formal equivalences. Re-entry requires evidence capable of changing a named edge,
observable, refusal, or cost verdict.

## Node and computation contract

Every node exposes:

```text
obstruction + admitted premises
  -> semantic equation sequence
  -> generated object or typed refusal
  -> certificate and error/cost law
  -> downstream consumer + explicit boundary.
```

Heavy algebra and numerics live in the computation workbench. A node retains only
the decisive equality needed to understand and verify the construction. One locked
command runs every retained certificate.

## What the calculus retains

The durable gain is not another formal encoding. It is the following reusable
obstruction locator:

```text
native action + preparation + observable
  -> generated physical closure
  -> observable-required algebra
  -> common internal return
  -> alternative realizations with one target
  -> correctness / information / cost verdicts kept separate.
```

This separates three failures that textbook presentations often conflate:

1. missing contact or grading data is a construction failure;
2. loss of coherent phase is an information failure; and
3. an unchanged expensive internal inverse or time integral is a computational
   failure.

The first two are handled constructively within `v1`. The third is exposed but not
solved.

## Horizon, propagation, and re-entry

The positive domain is finite carriers or Fock sectors, explicit UV/volume or bath
regulators, bounded perturbative order or certified finite inverses, the frozen
Pauli--Fierz bound/open interface, and the frozen Anderson equilibrium Green
function. It excludes continuum renormalization, thermodynamic and zero-resolution
limits, phase transitions, generic Keldysh dynamics, asymptotic scattering, and
universal Hamiltonian solution.

Nodes and manuscripts may cite `V_response-1` only for the bounded semantic
interface and its typed refusals. They must not promote it to universal scalar
compression or complete-route leverage.

Re-entry requires a new operation, not another example: a reusable reduction of
the analytic return must lower the complete cost of the same unchanged observable
queries against an expert baseline on at least two heterogeneous consumers. A
promising inquiry is a query-adapted projection, moment, or rational-return summary
tested on repeated Pauli--Fierz bound/open queries and Anderson imaginary-/real-
frequency queries. Until such a probe is frozen, no successor candidate exists.

# Representation and free-field construction boundary

Status: supported bounded result

The Poincare representation constructs an orbit and little-group fiber. A finite
Lorentz carrier becomes a field realization only through an explicit intertwiner or
symbol-complex cohomology calculation. For declared finite symmetric/exterior/
Clifford families, the worktable recovers the requested standard-momentum fiber and
constructs selected causal quotients, positive-energy images, and free CCR/CAR
fields.

This result is stronger than a textbook equation because it exposes which carrier,
gauge, source, and factorization operations are generated and which are supplied.
It is weaker than a dynamics theorem: representation theory does not select an
action, interaction, state, detector, or approximation.

The result is consumed by nodes 09--16. Countable completion, real/parity choices,
mixed symmetry, source-image density, and interacting higher-spin consistency
remain outside it.

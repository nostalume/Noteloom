# Field-to-observable boundary

Status: supported abstractly and on one regulated scalar model

Free representation data and local field realization construct admissible
one-particle content. An interacting Hamiltonian, preparation, and observable are
additional inputs. From them the departure map `B=QHJ` constructs the visible
measure `M_B=B^dagger E_Q B`, whose resolvent, Fourier, moments, and continuum
boundary feed several observables.

For the regulated scalar/two-level benchmark, the same field-derived radial measure
produces one bound/effective-mass coefficient and one finite-time emitted-boson
event. Operator/Feshbach and Duhamel bounds certify the ground and short-time
regimes. The result does not imply a long-time decay rate, exact dressed measure,
resonance, or scattering matrix.

Thus the field equation is an intermediate realization contract. The predictive
compression begins only after dynamics, preparation, observable, and error regime
are explicit.

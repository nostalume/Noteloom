# Construction-Origin Audit of the N10 Spine

Date: 2026-09-02  
Scope: `N10` through `N10v`, their executable packets, and their declared uses  
Question: does the present route internally construct a reusable mathematical tool,
or does it mainly formalize and verify objects already supplied in answer-bearing
form?

## Verdict

The N10 branch is neither a merely formal rewrite nor yet the general constructor
claimed by some of its prose. It has this actual shape:

```text
     executable returns theorem strings and fixed rewrite rules        [N10l]

  -> exterior-power transfer and generated gauge-wave factorization    [N10m]

     mixed-channel obstruction                                          [N10n]

  -> common null quotient and Fischer chain coincidence                  [N10p]

  -> factor-to-pushforward visible-measure compiler with inequivalent transfer [N10v].
```

Thus the branch now contains a **constructive compiler-and-repair kernel inside a
smaller formalistic shell**.
The kernel is valuable: it enumerates natural-map words inside a declared grammar,
computes residuals, solves exact rational repair equations, generates constraints,
returns refusal certificates, transfers across bosonic and Clifford grammars, and
is consumed by source, Euler, and causal calculations. The shell begins where an
answer-bearing presentation, grammar, route registry, multiplicity, or syzygy is
handwritten and then returned under the name “generated.”

The global paper must not claim that N10 already constructs local field machinery
from a physical representation. What is supported is narrower and more useful:

> Given one of two supplied symmetric carrier grammars, N10c--N10g construct a
> bounded constrained free-field machine by residual repair, retain its operations,
> report insufficient-resource obstructions, and pass regression, transfer, and
> bounded use checks. Carrier search and general grammar construction remain open.

## Audit vocabulary

The ledger uses four marks. They are not mutually exclusive because “used” is a
downstream role rather than an origin.

- `S` — **supplied** independently to the active construction. This is legitimate
  for empirical inputs, declared capabilities, resource bounds, group data, and
  irreducible theorem contracts. It is answer-bearing when it already contains the
  carrier, relation, route, coefficient, or identity the tool claims to construct.
- `D` — **derived** in mathematics or prose from prior objects. A derivation can be
  correct without leaving a reusable generator.
- `G` — **generated** by a retained operation whose inputs do not encode its answer,
  whose repair follows from a calculated obstruction, and whose output includes
  certificates or refusals.
- `U` — **used** by a later theorem, computation, observable, or route decision
  without reconstructing it from scratch.

The important distinction is not “supplied is bad.” The physical fiber, invariant
metric, Clifford action, capability request, and finite resource budget must enter
somewhere. The defect is an **answer-bearing supply** narrated as generation.

## The first formalistic shell has been dismantled

N10m implements the bounded **natural-operation compiler** proposed by the first
audit. It leaves N10h as a readable mathematical derivation and compatibility
adapter, while moving new construction to a law-driven interface.

Implemented interface:

```text
NaturalGrammar(
  free power carrier law,
  invariant tensors/actions,
  optional quadratic coefficient action)
 -> generated natural operations,
    typed shifts/degrees,
    relations computed from the functor laws,
    normal form/rewrite interface,
    bounded normal-form certificates,
    missing-resource or nonconfluence refusal.
```

### Independent inputs actually tested

- a free symmetric or exterior power on `V*`;
- universal functor operations, invariant tensors, and coefficient-module actions;
- the power exchange sign and metric-induced duality;
- no token list, pair-rule table, expected field coefficients, or route name.

### Construction result

1. Universal power multiplication, derivation, metric contraction, and optional
   coefficient action emit the admitted tokens and typed degrees.
2. Composite evaluation emits the commuting, anticommuting, trace, and nilpotence
   relations; neither old packet name is an input.
3. Closure, decreasing word order, and normalization idempotence pass for every
   emitted word of length at most three.
4. The symmetric and coefficient-action packets feed N10c/N10g and recover their
   existing equations and multiplier.
5. The exterior input generates `AP=-PA+Q`, `P^2=A^2=0`, omits the symmetric
   metric trace, and feeds a separate consumer that constructs
   `D=Q-PA`, `R=P`, `C=A` with `DR=0` and `D+RC=Q`.

### Benchmark verdict and new stop

All three original requirements now pass:

- **regression:** recover the present symmetric packet without a packet-specific
  branch;
- **transfer:** generate or meaningfully refuse the independent carrier without
  adding its expected relation table;
- **use:** a residual or compatibility constructor consumes the returned grammar.

N10m therefore owns the new front edge of the bounded construction spine, while
N10c becomes its residual-resolution backend. This is not yet carrier-global. The
compiler recognizes the universal operations of its programmed free-power laws;
it does not enumerate every invariant natural map of an arbitrary carrier
expression.

N10n closes the projected one-row form of that bench. The failure
`TPf=2Af` generates the only same-family trace repair and fixes
`R_r=P-UA/(2r+d-2)`. Its composition with `A` produces a rank-indexed rewrite,
which the flat N10m grammar could not express. An independent exact `O(3)`
intertwiner calculation gives multiplicities `(1,0,1)` into the raise, same, and
lower harmonic targets for ranks one through three. It also exposes a complementary
`2r+1`-dimensional mixed channel. A downstream consumer uses the new relation and
returns the unresolved residual factored through `A epsilon`.

N10o corrects the initial interpretation of that result. Trace freedom is an
optional irreducible-carrier presentation, not a consequence of the particle
representation. The complement in `V tensor H_r` and the actual residual are also
distinct: the latter is typed by `A epsilon in H_(s-2)`, so it generates either the
constraint `A epsilon=0` or a compensator `chi in H_(s-2)` with
`delta chi=A epsilon`. If trace freedom is not a requested capability, the third
honest branch is to rerun the full symmetric construction. The mixed carrier is
refused because no current residual lands in it.

N10p then applies the physical discriminator that was previously missing. At the
same four-dimensional null momentum, constrained harmonic, compensated harmonic,
and compressed symmetric complexes all have quotient dimension two and
transverse-screen rank two for spins two through five. The zero-order assembly
`Phi=phi+Uchi/(2s)` identifies the compensated carrier, gauge map, equation
kernel, and recovery with the compressed symmetric baseline. The constrained
branch saves `(s-1)^2` field components while removing exactly `(s-1)^2` null
gauge directions. Thus projected-carrier construction is real, but neither branch
adds free physical content or demonstrated computation reduction.

N10q applies the first off-shell discriminator instead of opening another carrier.
The restricted gauge pairing generates the source condition
`R^dagger j in im A^dagger`; the compensated pairing generates
`R^dagger j+A^dagger k=0`. Exact rational harmonic calculations for spins two
through five construct a section `AS=1`, the slice
`sigma(phi,chi)=phi-RSchi`, and its dual source lift. These operations identify the
two source quotients and preserve the same gauge-invariant curvature response away
from zero momentum. They also expose the decisive cost: `S` has inverse momentum
degree, and no polynomial-local `S` can exist because `A(0)S(0)` vanishes. The
gauge-slice transport is therefore nonlocal and cannot by itself be a cheaper local
causal source machine. This does not rule out a separately generated constrained
Euler/Green operation.

N10r constructs that missing operation directly. Fischer adjunction makes
`D=Q-RA` self-adjoint. Raised transverse layers close the divergence recurrence,
isolate one gauge layer, and generate the quotient inverse
`Q^(-1)f_s(RA/Q)`. Exact spins two through five give polynomial degree `s`, Green
depth `s+1`, and declared load `(s+1)^3`, exceeding the compensated depth one and
load `2s^2+2`. The generic gain claim is rejected on this metric; a source prepared
in one invariant layer remains a conditional one-Green route.

N10s tests that condition without receiving a layer label. The retained selector
calculates harmonic and divergence residuals, short-circuits when `B j=0`, and
otherwise constructs the N10r spectral projectors. It discovers that N10e's
nonzero-screen current has layers `0,2`. The failed single-layer condition then
generates a different input carrier: pair antisymmetry, pair exchange, and trace
freedom are forced on a compact two-derivative seed, producing a Weyl-typed double
divergence source in layer `2` with nonzero screen. This is a genuine transfer and
use result. Its cost result is negative: trace freedom simultaneously makes the
compensated adapter the identity, so both presentations perform the same active
one-Green solve and curvature recovery.

N10t changes the primitive computational target from field response to the named
curvature. The N10j annihilators generate `K_sR=0`, which removes every raised
N10r layer and normalizes both constrained and compensated routes to `G_QK_sJ`.
The retained compiler caches this response once per source for every curvature
functional and fuses derivative/Green symbols in spectral implementations to avoid
an unnecessary large intermediate. This is a genuine generative and computational
tool relative to full-field execution. It is not evidence for a preferred carrier:
the normal form and reuse are common to both presentations, and channel counts are
kept formal until a concrete discretization supplies active coordinates and runtime.

N10u changes the output type again: from a classical curvature response to the
positive spectral measure seen by a prepared quantum detector. The executable does
not receive a density exponent. It derives the radial shell power `d-2`, adds twice
the generated curvature degree, and returns `2s+1` in three spatial dimensions.
The detector contraction, smearing, gap, and coupling are correctly marked supplied
because symmetry cannot choose an apparatus. Its angular coefficient is no longer
supplied: covariance, irreducibility, and trace generate `4pi/(2s+1)` from the
detector's chiral norm. Vacuum one-particle selection, angular/helicity quotienting,
and reuse of the same measure for a ground shift and an emitted event are genuine
downstream generation and use. The result remains an effective free detector
through order `g^2`, not an interacting higher-spin matter theory.

N10v tests whether this is a retained tool rather than a formula. Its interface
accepts orbit/Hilbert, observable, preparation, and energy factor maps, but no
expected density or threshold exponent. Multiplication constructs the radial
departure measure; a checked monotone pushforward constructs its spectral density.
The same operation recovers both N10u's massless quintic curvature channel and
N9c's nonlinear massive recoil channel with a square-root threshold, then supplies
bound and open transforms in both. An unpartitioned nonmonotone energy returns a
refusal. This is genuine regression, inequivalent transfer, downstream use, and
failure behavior inside the declared radial one-particle class.

## Consequence for manuscript composition

N10v now supports a manuscript composition pass around the visible-measure
generator. It does not authorize a novelty or finite-coupling claim. The present
free-field results remain valuable as construction substrate and regression
material, and the thesis must respect the origin ledger:

- N10m is the free-power compiler, N10n is the projected-operation compiler, and
  N10o decides whether that projected presentation is authorized and how its
  residual is repaired; N10p demotes the compensated route to a Fischer
  reparameterization and the constrained route to a conditional partial gauge;
  N10q then demotes gauge-slice **transport** for local causal-source use because
  its source and field equivalence maps require an inverse-momentum section; N10r
  generates the direct response and rejects its generic load advantage; N10s then
  constructs a single-layer preparation but rejects dominance over the same-source
  compensated route; N10t then removes all curvature-invisible response layers and
  retains the common observable compiler rather than promoting either carrier;
  N10u then consumes that common curvature and constructs a cached quantum measure
  with same-object bound/open use, without reversing N10t's presentation-neutral
  verdict; N10v generates the angular normalization and transfers the factor-to-
  pushforward interface to N9c's massive channel; N10c--N10g remain the retained residual/source/action backends where their flat
  grammars apply;
- N10h remains the mathematical derivation and legacy packet adapter, not the
  executable generator;
- N10i must be presented as selection over a fixed certified registry;
- N10j's invariant curvature map may remain a generated spin-indexed operation, but
  its multiplicity routine is a regression certificate, not a search engine;
- N10l's source complex and syzygies belong to a theorem/derivation layer until its
  maps are emitted and applied by a real constructor;
- textbook recovery belongs to regression evidence, while the negative cost result
  is a genuine global conclusion.

The projected-carrier free-shell, generic source-response, first spin-two
preparation, and free repeated-curvature horizons are closed. Single-layer emission
or repeated curvature queries are no longer sufficient re-entry conditions. A new
branch must supply a different observable, dynamics that does not commute with the
curvature map, or concrete discretization evidence that changes active solve work
or conditioning. Mixed symmetry remains parked until such an obstruction or
requested capability lands there.

# Soft-gravity boundary contracts

These primary sources delimit the interpretation of node 22. They establish
both a perturbative coherent-state route and a serious obstruction to treating that
route as the universal asymptotic foundation. They do not turn the one-loop
logarithm into an exact infraparticle theorem.

## Ware--Saotome--Akhoury 2013

- Primary source: J. Ware, R. Saotome, and R. Akhoury, *Construction of an
  asymptotic S matrix for perturbative quantum gravity*,
  [arXiv:1308.6285](https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.6285).
- Hypotheses used here: perturbative quantum gravity in its soft regime and
  Faddeev--Kulish-type asymptotic dynamics.
- Contracted output: an asymptotic operator constructs coherent gravitationally
  dressed states and an infrared-finite S matrix; the soft part of scalar
  scattering admits an all-orders treatment.
- Research use: theorem/evidence contract that coherent dressing is a legitimate
  candidate when the bare Fock pole meets a soft-graviton continuum.
- Boundary: it does not prove that node 22's normalized bubble is the complete
  dressed scalar spectral measure or fix this worktable's local counterterms.

## Choi--Akhoury 2019

- Primary source: S. Choi and R. Akhoury, *Subleading soft dressings of asymptotic
  states in QED and perturbative quantum gravity*,
  [arXiv:1907.05438](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.05438).
- Hypotheses used here: perturbative QED/gravity, soft expansion through subleading
  order, and asymptotic-symmetry charge conservation.
- Contracted output: dressed amplitudes correlate soft and hard sectors; their
  virtual part agrees with the infrared-finite part of the traditional amplitude,
  and the dressed construction reproduces inclusive cross sections.
- Research use: candidate rejoin edge from node 22's continuum kernel to an
  inclusive observable without restoring an unsupported bare pole.
- Boundary: the result does not supply this project's graph-orbit compiler,
  spin-two numerator quotient, or finite-resolution detector automatically.

## Campiglia--Laddha 2015

- Primary source: M. Campiglia and A. Laddha, *Asymptotic symmetries of gravity and
  soft theorems for massive particles*,
  [arXiv:1509.01406](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.01406).
- Hypotheses used here: asymptotically flat gravity with massive scalar scattering
  data and generalized BMS transformations.
- Contracted output: Ward identities acting on massive scalar and graviton
  scattering data are related to leading and subleading soft-graviton theorems.
- Research use: evidence that the massive scalar's soft sector is controlled by an
  asymptotic symmetry operation, not an unrelated infrared patch.
- Boundary: soft Ward identities constrain asymptotics but do not by themselves
  construct a spectral pole, a detector, or a renormalized self-energy.

## Prabhu--Satishchandran--Wald 2022

- Primary source: K. Prabhu, G. Satishchandran, and R. M. Wald, *Infrared Finite
  Scattering Theory in Quantum Field Theory and Quantum Gravity*,
  [arXiv:2203.14334](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14334).
- Hypotheses used here: scattering with massless gauge/gravitational fields,
  asymptotic memory, and the charge structure of candidate in/out spaces.
- Contracted output: standard Fock states do not represent memory sectors; the
  authors prove an obstruction to gravitational Faddeev--Kulish states as
  supertranslation-charge eigenstates and motivate an algebraic formulation of
  infrared-finite scattering.
- Research use: coherent dressing cannot be installed as the unique successor to
  node 22, which instead constructs a resolution-indexed observable algebra first
  and asks competing state-space realizations to reproduce it.
- Boundary: this source does not prove node 22's finite-window formula, identify its
  1PI kernel with a gauge-invariant detector, or rule out every perturbative use of
  coherent dressings.

## Live comparison

Ware--Saotome--Akhoury and Choi--Akhoury support perturbative coherent-state
constructions and infrared-finite amplitudes. Prabhu--Satishchandran--Wald challenge
their interpretation as a fundamental gravitational asymptotic Hilbert space.
The worktable records this as an unresolved realization choice, not a literature
vote. The shared benchmark is whether each route reconstructs node 22's same nested
finite-resolution effects and response functional.

## Yennie--Frautschi--Suura 1961

- Primary source: D. R. Yennie, S. C. Frautschi, and H. Suura, *The infrared
  divergence phenomena and high-energy processes*,
  [Annals of Physics 13 (1961)](https://doi.org/10.1016/0003-4916(61)90151-8).
- Hypotheses consumed: perturbative QED, separation of soft photons from a hard
  process, and inclusive summation over unresolved radiation.
- Contracted output: real and virtual infrared factors are organized by the same
  external charged-particle data; inclusive probabilities cancel regulator
  dependence order by order.
- Research use: node 31 may generate the leading virtual complement from the same
  positive emission kernel used for unresolved and resolved detector bins.
- Boundary: this does not supply node 31's finite loop remainder, its detector
  geometry, or its `2+1`-dimensional Laurent coefficients.

## Weinberg 1965

- Primary source: S. Weinberg, *Infrared Photons and Gravitons*,
  [Physical Review 140, B516](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.140.B516).
- Hypotheses consumed: soft massless quanta attached to external lines of a hard
  scattering process.
- Contracted output: the leading soft factor is determined by external momenta and
  charges, and the real/virtual infrared singularities cancel in inclusive rates.
- Research use: the endpoint current in node 31 is an operation on the generated
  hard event, not a new diagram-specific numerator.
- Boundary: dimensional phase space changes the divergence power; the familiar
  four-dimensional logarithm is not imported into the present `2+1` bench.

## Kulish--Faddeev 1970

- Primary source: P. P. Kulish and L. D. Faddeev, *Asymptotic conditions and
  infrared divergences in quantum electrodynamics*,
  [Theoretical and Mathematical Physics 4](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/tmf4141).
- Hypotheses consumed: long-range QED interactions and asymptotic charged states.
- Contracted output: the free Fock asymptotic dynamics is inadequate and can be

- Research use: dressing is retained as a competing completion after the

- Boundary: node 31 constructs a first-order detector instrument, not dressed
  asymptotic Hilbert spaces or an all-orders scattering operator.

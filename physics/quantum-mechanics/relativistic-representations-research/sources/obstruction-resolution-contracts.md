## `OR-01` — A Poincare module can generate a gauge system

- Source: K. B. Alkalaev, M. Grigoriev, and I. Yu. Tipunin,
  [Massless Poincare modules and gauge invariant equations](https://arxiv.org/abs/0811.3999).
- Locator: abstract and Sections 2--4, as already extracted in
  [polynomial-gauge contracts](polynomial-gauge-contracts.md).
- Contract: for the class of indecomposable massless Poincare modules treated by
  the authors, a parent/BRST construction produces a local gauge theory; reductions
  recover Labastida and unfolded formulations, and a separate momentum-space check
  compares gauge-inequivalent solutions with the Wigner module.
- Boundary for node 14: this establishes a module-to-gauge construction before the
  present work. It does not, from the source contract inspected so far, supply
  node 14's fixed-carrier evaluation-cokernel test, characteristic-debt ledger, or
  capability-relative Pareto selection.

## `OR-02` — Auxiliary-field elimination has locality and equivalence costs

- Source: Maxim Grigoriev,
  [Parent formulations, frame-like Lagrangians, and generalized auxiliary fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.1793).
- Locator: Introduction; Sections 3--4 on the parent construction and generalized
  auxiliary fields; Section 5 on totally symmetric fields.
- Contract: parent formulations relate Lagrangian, unfolded, and frame-like forms
  by different reductions; the allowed field space matters, and eliminating an
  infinite collection of generalized auxiliary fields can involve unbounded
  derivative order or locality subtleties.
- Research use: a contractible or auxiliary pair cannot be assigned zero semantic
  cost merely because cohomology is unchanged. node 14 must record carrier, derivative,
  inverse-locality, and recovery costs.
- Boundary: the source begins with a gauge theory or parent system and studies its
  reductions. node 14's candidate direction begins with a particle fiber and declared
  local resources, then computes failed lifts and residuals.

## `OR-03` — BGG detour machinery generates gauge and dynamical complexes

- Source: A. R. Gover, E. Latini, and A. Waldron,
  [Metric projective geometry, BGG detour complexes and partially massless gauge theories](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.6778).
- Locator: abstract; a section-level extraction remains open before any detailed
  theorem from the paper is used.
- Contract: projective BGG machinery combined with Yang--Mills detour complexes
  generates invariant gauge theories, including gauge and constraint operators;
  curved versions expose obstructions to higher-spin propagation on Einstein
  backgrounds.
- Research use: prevents node 14 from presenting “complexes generated by
  representation theory” as new in itself.
- Boundary: the inspected contract does not identify node 14's flat
  little-group-fiber recovery problem, finite evaluation map, or proposed cost
  partial order with a BGG construction.

## `OR-04` — Compatibility complexes generate all identities of a given operator

- Source: Igor Khavkine,
  [Compatibility complexes of overdetermined PDEs of finite type, with applications to the Killing equation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.03751).
- Locator: abstract and the paper's construction of complete compatibility
  operators; detailed curved examples are outside node 14's current bench.
- Contract: for a regular finite-type operator in the treated setting, a complete
  compatibility operator generates all differential operators annihilating it,
  and the construction extends to a full compatibility complex. For the Killing
  operator, the first compatibility operator generates local gauge-invariant
  observables.
- Research use: node 14 may use compatibility completion downstream after a gauge map
  or equation has been constructed.
- Boundary: compatibility theory starts with an operator and constructs its
  universal identities. node 14 asks the reverse bounded design question: which
  operator complex realizes a supplied orbit fiber under a declared resource
  budget?

## `OR-05` — The fermionic unconstrained formulation has two auxiliary ranks

- Source: D. Francia and A. Sagnotti,
  [Minimal Local Lagrangians for Higher-Spin Geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0507144).
- Locator: abstract and the symmetric spinor-tensor construction; detailed action
  coefficients remain owned by `AP-03`.
- Contract: a local unconstrained formulation for a symmetric rank-`n`
  spinor-tensor uses a rank-`n-2` compensator and, for a Lagrangian presentation,
  a rank-`n-3` multiplier.
- Research use: this externally bounds the carrier ranks in node 14's comparison.
  node 14 independently constructs the compensator equation complex from
  `SP=-P^2Gamma` and the triple-gamma carrier residual, then proves its quotient
  equivalence by a gamma-Fischer gauge slice.
- Boundary: the source does not establish node 14's two-layer equation-target
  compression or its capability-relative cost verdict. Conversely, node 14's
  equation complex does not establish the source's local action or current
  exchange without the multiplier.

## Current source verdict

The primary sources already cover:

```text
Poincare module -> parent/BRST gauge theory,
given theory -> parent/unfolded/frame-like reductions,
geometric representation data -> BGG detour gauge complexes,
given differential operator -> universal compatibility complex.
```

The half-integer regression also confirms that constrained and unconstrained
spinor-tensor presentations are established neighboring constructions. Its
candidate contribution is narrower: the residual-first generation, the exact
gamma-Fischer quotient map, and the proof that two—rather than one or three—target
layers suffice for free realization.

The presently unverified candidate is the combined route

```text
little-group fiber plus a finite local-resource budget
  -> evaluation and syzygy residuals in Lorentz multiplicity spaces
  -> characteristic/fiber recovery
  -> nondominated realization complexes under a capability cost.
```

Do not call that route novel until a deeper comparison with algebraic free
resolutions, Spencer cohomology, BRST/BV, and BGG constructions either separates
its theorem or shows that it is a reformulation of an existing one.

## MG-01 — Green-hyperbolic operator contract

Christian Baer, [Green-hyperbolic operators on globally hyperbolic
spacetimes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.0738), *Communications in Mathematical
Physics* 333 (2015), 1585-1615,
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-014-2097-7).

- **Hypotheses:** a linear differential operator `P` between vector-bundle sections
  on a globally hyperbolic spacetime, together with advanced and retarded Green
  operators for `P` and its formal adjoint.
- **Output used:** two-sided inverse identities on compactly supported sections,
  causal support, uniqueness, extensions to one-sided compact supports, and the
  exact causal sequence generated by `E=G^+-G^-`.
- **Semantic bridge:** a boundary prescription is part of the operator; a rational
  inverse symbol alone is not a Green operator.
- **Boundary:** this contract does not make a gauge-degenerate operator
  Green-hyperbolic and does not select a quantum state or Feynman inverse.

## MG-02 — Maxwell vector-potential quotient

Marco Benini, [Relative Cauchy evolution for the vector potential on globally
hyperbolic spacetimes](https://doi.org/10.2140/memocs.2015.3.177), *Mathematics
and Mechanics of Complex Systems* 3 (2015), 177-226.

- **Hypotheses:** differential forms on an oriented, time-oriented, globally
  hyperbolic spacetime; exact gauge transformations `A -> A+d chi`; the
  Laplace--de Rham completion `P=delta d+d delta`.
- **Output used:** `P` is Green-hyperbolic; its Green operators commute with `d`
  and `delta`; every Maxwell gauge class has a Lorenz representative; the causal
  propagator induces

  ```text
  ker(delta|Omega_c^1)/delta d Omega_c^1
    -> {spacelike-compact Maxwell solutions}/d Omega_sc^0;
  ```

  and the source pairing `<J,E J'>` descends to the quotient.
- **Independent check:** Sections 2B, 3, and 4 explicitly distinguish the
  non-hyperbolic Maxwell operator `delta d`, its hyperbolic completion, the causal
  propagator, and the observable quotient.
- **Boundary:** topological sectors and alternative notions of gauge equivalence
  can change degeneracy and locality properties.

## MG-03 — support cohomology and topology contract

Marco Benini, [Optimal space of linear classical observables for Maxwell
`k`-forms via spacelike and timelike compact de Rham
cohomologies](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.7563), 2014.

- **Hypotheses:** globally hyperbolic spacetime and the stated compact,
  spacelike-compact, or timelike-compact de Rham complexes.
- **Output used:** support-sensitive cohomology controls whether Maxwell
  observables separate gauge classes and whether redundant or topological sectors
  remain.
- **Semantic bridge:** the quotient is not only pointwise gauge removal; support
  and topology determine its global completion.
- **Boundary:** N4e specializes to topologically trivial Minkowski spacetime and
  does not transfer nondegeneracy claims to arbitrary backgrounds.

## Admission rule

packet below or a bounded theorem contract naming hypotheses, output, and failure
boundary. Sources validate inputs and contracts; they do not replace the internal
semantic computation required by a research claim.

## Open source gaps

- Numerically normalized crossed diagonal `cos(2 beta phi)` form factors at
  `xi=1/5`, with finite-volume and continuum remainder bounds sufficient for the
  N4x stability radius and local-overlap inequality.
- A complete operator-algebraic matrix-valued sine-Gordon construction with
  strongly commuting wedge fields, nontrivial double-cone algebras, and a local
  spectral projector for `B_1`.
- A more precise modern Mackey theorem contract if N2 requires measure-theoretic
  detail beyond its current Wigner/Bekaert–Boulanger boundary.
- A classification theorem for nonchiral tensor and potential complexes with
  prescribed little-group cohomology; mixed symmetry remains open.
- Primary massive Proca/Fierz–Pauli comparison and normalization sources beyond
  the internally constructed massless symmetric complexes.
- A rigorous comparison of free-equation equivalence with interacting
  inconsistency for N7.
- Density of compact projected-conserved symmetric-potential sources in the full
  future-shell `L2` helicity-pair space; support faithfulness is proved, density is
  not.

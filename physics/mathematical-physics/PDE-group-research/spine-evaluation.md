# Spine evaluation

## Verdict

The feasible research object is a bounded reduction compiler, not a universal
PDE--group inverse:

```text
represented filtered operator algebra = candidate-generating middle language
ReductionWitness                    = capability-bearing result
```

A useful constructor returns analysis, synthesis, reduced dynamics, recovery,
residual/error, validity, and whole-route cost for a named observable—or a typed
obstruction. A Lie group is optional output after algebraic closure and global
hypotheses, never a required PDE-first input.

## Capability audit

| Axis | Supported now | Boundary / debt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| bilateral meaning | independent representation-first and PDE-first routes meet at comparable witnesses | coincidence is equivalence for one observable, not unique inversion |
| internal construction | factor, centralizer, cyclic, quotient, complex, mode-bundle, defect-kernel, coefficient-algebra, and projector routes | completeness is grammar-, domain-, and budget-relative |
| executable unity | common witness/decision envelopes preserve route-specific certificates | automatic discovery remains confined to admitted finite schemas |
| analytical computation | selected oscillator/Coulomb spectra, finite-time and fixed-accuracy bounds, Pauli/adjoint heat blocks, and finite-window propagation | most domain, completeness, convergence, and global-integration claims remain theorem contracts |
| human computation | kernels, projectors, quotient sectors, recurrences, and pulled-back effects replace component catalogues | empirical human-time validation is still open |
| representation depth | scalar, bundle, spinor, outer-multiplicity, commuting, and noncommutative finite algebras are represented | strongest executable cases are low-rank, structured, and mostly self-adjoint |
| refusal | broken symmetry/domain, gap closing, no cyclic gain, unsupported grammar, and exhausted budget are explicit | refusal coverage is not uniform across every formal calculus |

## What is genuinely constructed

This is more than an optimization layer. The workbench constructs objects that
were not supplied in the input:

- effective algebras from factors, commutators, stabilizer defects, or coefficient
  relations;
- ineffective-ideal quotients, central projectors, isotypic multiplicities, and
  primitive carriers;
- differential actions and reduced channels from represented algebra data;
- quotient domains, projector jets, leakage arrows, cyclic/active modules, and
  observable recovery maps;
- exact residuals, error bounds, costs, and refusal certificates.

Optimization begins only after those constructions are compared with a baseline
for the same observable. G16 is an important negative result: lower semantic depth
did not yield lower sampled runtime because both routes still exponentiated the
full carrier. G17 changed the construction by building the preparation-reachable
carrier, after which reuse—not one-shot execution—produced the measured gain.

## Decisive evidence families

| Family | What it establishes | What it does not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| quadratic and Coulomb | factor/centralizer/cyclic routes can be constructed and compared exactly | general PDE solvability |
| boundary and quotient PDE | symmetry reduction can end in a lower-dimensional PDE with descended strata and accuracy cost | Cartesian separability or universal gain |
| Pauli/spinor | Clifford and curvature data support bilateral channel coincidence and bounded global promotion | arbitrary spin geometry |
| adjoint/multiplicity | natural intertwiners, PDE-first closure, covariance action, and one heat block are constructible | unique global group or full regular spectrum |
| natural tensors/coupled carriers | relation-derived seeds and defect quotients avoid raw candidate enumeration | group-free completeness beyond the admitted relation grammar |
| coefficient and star algebras | exact projectors, center, isotypic blocks, multiplicity, and primitive carriers are constructed without preferred eigenvectors | scalable arbitrary-dimensional decomposition |
| variable projectors | coefficient data generate `P,P',P''`, invariant leakage arrows, and finite-window observables | a global trivialization or several-coordinate flatness |
| active carriers | pointwise and coefficient-family modules preserve the same observable and win after sufficient reuse | one-shot dominance or protection from ambient regrowth |

## Feasibility boundary

The program may claim:

- constructive reductions within declared algebraic/geometric grammars;
- analytic conclusions only under matched domain and theorem contracts;
- computational leverage only when whole-route same-observable cost is lower;
- human leverage when a compact generator replaces exhaustive sectors and retains
  reconstruction;
- explicit negative information when a candidate or promotion fails.

It may not claim a useful group for every PDE, a bijective group--PDE
correspondence, solvability from formal closure, generic nonlinear decomposition,
or universal route dominance.

## Adjusted frontier

G18 resolves pointwise repetition for finite coefficient families: one exact
module is preserved by every specialization, but its leverage is conditional on
window size and it may regrow to the ambient carrier. A constant tensor lift is
already formal once its common-core hypotheses hold. G19 now has both a general
witness shell for supplied constant-rank frames and one non-Pauli polynomial
transfer in which the frame and rank region are derived from two G18 provenance
expressions. The transfer separates potential invariance from full-PDE invariance:
its flat in-bundle connection coexists with exact angular leakage. Factoring that
leakage constructs a local infinitesimal rotation and represented internal action,
while the boundary refuses a global domain symmetry.

The reusable coefficient-jet provenance grammar is now frozen and has exact
constant regression, non-Pauli transfer, rank-jump, and coefficient-escape
controls. The rank control exposes an information boundary: finite jets alone
cannot determine neighborhood rank, even for polynomial continuations. Exact
whole-polynomial data, a degree/remainder bound, or a pointwise-only output is
therefore mandatory.

That executable G19 slice now feeds an executable G20 constructor. Rank-one
first-order residual row space constructs a real projective derivation line; one
full polynomial commutator kernel separately decides whether it has a visible
constant skew-Hermitian lift. Public and direct tests distinguish successful lift,
the same line destroyed by `xI`, rank-two and coefficient-only nonselection,
complex/no-real-form, higher-order, covariance, and bounded exhaustion. The next
feasible action is a multi-line bracket atlas modulo internal ambiguity, followed
—not merged—by integration and domain flow. Controlled leakage, observable
propagation, and boundary descent remain downstream obligations. The completed
local bridge lives in the
[G20 owner](research-graph.md#resolved-local-bridge-g20-residual-direction-to-infinitesimal-lie-lift).

Human-cost experiments re-enter only through a blinded reconstruction task.
Several-coordinate geometry re-enters only with a curvature/path compatibility
probe. Analytic Pauli work re-enters only when boundary, variable curvature,
noncompact carrier, or a projector-kernel observable changes the current verdict.

Further scalar examples, polynomial catalogues, arbitrary dimension, global bundle
integration, unbounded domains, and nonlinear systems stay parked until they
expose a failure of the common witness or change the bounded capability claim. The
authoritative dependency and re-entry view is the [research graph](research-graph.md).

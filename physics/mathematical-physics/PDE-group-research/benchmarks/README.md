# Benchmark index

Benchmarks are replayable evidence packets, not an example-driven research spine.
Each packet tests a frozen construction, transfer, refusal, analytic promotion, or
whole-route cost claim. Mathematical ownership remains in [research nodes](../nodes/README.md);
executable entrypoints remain in the [computation workbench](../computation/README.md).

## Representation to differential realization

- [Legendre / SO(3)](legendre-so3.md) and [rank-one symmetric spaces](rank-one-symmetric-spaces.md): scalar regression and transfer boundary.
- [`p`-forms on spheres](p-form-sphere.md): bundle-valued spectrum.
- [`SO(3)` tensor operators](tensor-operator-so3.md), [`SU(3)` tensor operators](tensor-operator-su3.md), and [adjoint multiplicity PDE](su-adjoint-multiplicity-pde.md): finite multiplicity and differential realization.

## Adversarial boundary

- [bounded refusal probe](refusal-probe.md): unsupported grammar and budget failure must return a typed obstruction rather than a guessed group.

## Admission rule

A new packet must state the target claim, independent dimension tested, inputs,
operation, witness/error, provenance, resource model, permitted consequence, and
failure boundary. A regression cannot promote transfer; transfer cannot prove a
cost gain; a cost failure does not invalidate mathematical correctness.

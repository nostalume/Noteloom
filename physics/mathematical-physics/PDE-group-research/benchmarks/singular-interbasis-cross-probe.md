# Singular oscillator: polynomial–Lie interbasis cross-probe

## 1. Spine binding and bounded horizon

- **Upstream anchor:** the PDE-generated quadratic presentation and positive
  Jacobi module from the singular-oscillator polynomial transfer.
- **Bridge question:** do that nonlinear-algebra route and an independently
  constructed parent-Lie route produce the same normalized transformation between
  two separation-integral eigenbases?
- **Invariant target:** on one energy eigenspace `H=E_N`, the phase-insensitive
  probabilities `|<n_B|q_A>|^2` between the Cartesian integral `B` and angular
  integral `A`.
- **Special resources:** isotropic confinement, two positive half-line
  singular-oscillator modules, simple finite spectra, and the exact `N<=4`
  certificate budget.
- **Rejoin edge:** equality of primitive Jacobi data, spectra, and probability
  matrices, followed by a whole-route verdict.
- **Stop condition:** establish the same finite observable without coordinate
  wavefunction expansion. Do not catalogue the resulting orthogonal polynomials.

Source [39](../sources.md) supplies the theorem contract that this system has
finite quadratic-algebra models and separation-integral eigenbases. The two
constructions and their equality witness below are internal.

## 2. Polynomial route

The preceding constructor starts only from the PDE coefficients. It generates
`H,A,B`, their nonlinear relations, and on `H=E_N` the Jacobi data

```text
b_n=2n+5/2,
a_n=E_N b_n-b_n^2-1/4,
u_(n+1)^2=4(n+1)(n+5/2)(N-n)(N-n+5/2).                 (1)
```

Thus `B` is diagonal and `A` is irreducibly tridiagonal in the positive finite
module. The route has not named a group.

## 3. Independent factor/enveloping route

Factor the two half-line Hamiltonians. Their positive discrete `su(1,1)` modules
have lowest weights

```text
k_x=(1+nu_x)/2=5/4,       k_y=(1+nu_y)/2=7/4,          (2)
```

and intrinsic actions

```text
K_0|n>=(n+k)|n>,
K_+|n>=sqrt((n+1)(n+2k)) |n+1>,
K_-|n>=sqrt(n(n+2k-1)) |n-1>.                         (3)
```

This route constructs rather than assumes the group-representation data: the
indicial exponents determine `k_x,k_y`, and the factor relations determine (3).
On the fixed-total-excitation carrier `|n,N-n>`, form the coproduct generators and
Casimir

```text
K_a^Delta=K_a^x+K_a^y,
C_Delta=(K_0^Delta)^2-K_0^Delta-K_+^Delta K_-^Delta.   (4)
```

Direct substitution into the already generated physical operators gives

```text
B=2K_0^x,
A=2C_Delta+1/2-(g_x+g_y)/2.                            (5)
```

Equations (2)--(5), evaluated on `|n,N-n>`, return exactly (1): the off-diagonal
term is the product of one `x` raising and one `y` lowering coefficient. This is
the structural relation between the polynomial algebra and its parent Lie
representation in this problem—the polynomial generator is a composite Casimir,
not a new linear Lie generator.

The tensor-product decomposition has coupled lowest weights
`k_x+k_y+q`, `q=0,...,N`. Consequently

```text
lambda_q(A)
 =2(k_x+k_y+q)(k_x+k_y+q-1)+1/2-(g_x+g_y)/2.           (6)
```

The executable probe independently checks that every value (6) is a zero of the
monic characteristic recurrence constructed from (1).

## 4. Normalized overlap observable without eigenvector expansion

Let `P_n(lambda)` be generated by the Jacobi recurrence, with `P_0=1`. For a
simple eigenvalue `lambda_q`, the squared component of its normalized eigenvector
is constructed directly as

```text
w_nq = [P_n(lambda_q)^2/(u_1^2...u_n^2)]
       / sum_j [P_j(lambda_q)^2/(u_1^2...u_j^2)].       (7)
```

Equation (7) is exactly `|<n_B|q_A>|^2`. It removes arbitrary signs of both basis
vectors, which are not visible to the requested observable. Because both routes
construct identical `a_n,u_n^2,lambda_q`, they produce the same `w_nq` before any
special-function realization is chosen.

At `N=1`, the common result is

```text
W = [[7/12, 5/12],
     [5/12, 7/12]].                                    (8)
```

At `N=2`, the probe returns

```text
lambda(A)=(17/2,41/2,73/2),

W = [[3/8,  15/32, 5/32],
     [5/12, 1/48,  9/16],
     [5/24, 49/96, 9/32]].                             (9)
```

Every row and column sums exactly to one. Equations (8)--(9) are compact
certificates, not the derivation; the retained operation is (7).

## 5. Coincidence and cost verdict

The two composites have the same typed endpoint:

```text
PDE -> polynomial centralizer -> positive Jacobi module -> W,
PDE -> two su(1,1) factors -> coproduct Casimir        -> W.            (10)
```

The executable equality witness checks energy, `B` lattice, `A` diagonal,
off-diagonal norms, `A` spectrum, every entry of `W`, and both normalizations.
This replaces the previously open coordinate-overlap calculation: simple spectra
and the operator identifications (5) prove that the two finite eigenbases are the
Cartesian and angular separation bases, while (7) computes their phase-insensitive
overlap.

Neither complete route dominates:

- the factor route is shorter when the Cartesian split is already available and
  additionally exposes the parent Lie representation and tensor coupling;
- the polynomial route pays for a six-column complete centralizer and closure,
  but discovers both integrals without prior coordinates or a group and returns a
  relative-completeness certificate.

The decision is therefore `ParetoRetainBoth`, not a forced winner. After discovery,
both share the same `O(N^2)` exact probability evaluation from the Jacobi data.

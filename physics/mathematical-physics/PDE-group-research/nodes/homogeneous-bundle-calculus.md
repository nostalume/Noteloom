# Homogeneous-bundle representation calculus

## Capability and obstruction

The scalar spherical bench removes every multiplicity space by selecting the
trivial `K`-type.  That makes the first bridge readable, but it cannot represent
spin, polarization, differential forms, internal indices, or coupled invariant
systems.  The smallest repair is not a component-valued spherical function.  It is
the homogeneous bundle generated by a nontrivial representation of the stabilizer.

This node constructs a reusable reduction

```text
(G,K,tau, invariant differential arrows)
  -> finite representation multiplicity spaces
  -> finite operator/chain-complex blocks
  -> spectrum and observable recovery.
```

The compact case is the present horizon.  Noncompact direct integrals require a
separate Plancherel and domain certificate.

Material bindings are the local exterior-derivative construction in
`../differential-geometry.typ`, the chain-complex language in `../homology.typ`,
and entries 16–18 of the
[source map](../sources.md#homogeneous-bundles-and-differential-form-spectra).

## Typed input

Let `G` be a compact connected Lie group, `K` a closed subgroup, and

```text
tau: K -> U(W_tau)
```

a finite-dimensional unitary representation.  The associated bundle is

```text
E_tau = G x_K W_tau -> G/K.
```

A section is represented without coordinates by an equivariant function

```text
H_tau^infinity
  = {f:G -> W_tau smooth | f(gk)=tau(k)^(-1)f(g)}.
```

Left translation supplies the induced `G`-representation.  The remaining input is
a family of `G`-equivariant differential operators between such bundles, their
formal adjoints and closed realizations, and the observable to recover.

## Constructor 1: branching produces the visible blocks

Peter-Weyl decomposition followed by Frobenius reciprocity gives the theorem
contract

```text
L^2(E_tau)
  ~= completed direct sum over pi in G-hat of V_pi tensor M_(pi,tau),

M_(pi,tau) = Hom_K(V_pi restricted to K, W_tau).
```

The multiplicity space is the visible part of `V_pi` selected by the fiber type.
Its dimension is computed by a branching rule; no basis of bundle components is
required.  The spherical case is recovered by `tau=1`, where
`M_(pi,1)=V_pi^K`.

Let `D:H_tau^infinity -> H_sigma^infinity` be `G`-equivariant.  On the smooth
`G`-finite core, Schur's lemma forces

```text
D = direct sum over pi of I_(V_pi) tensor D_pi,

D_pi: M_(pi,tau) -> M_(pi,sigma).
```

Thus an invariant PDE acts only on multiplicity spaces.  Scalar spherical analysis
is the special case `dim M_(pi,tau)<=1`; the general result is finite-block rather
than scalar reduction.

## Constructor 2: differential arrows become a finite complex

Suppose the bundles form a graded family `E_p` and equivariant differential arrows
satisfy

```text
d_p: Gamma(E_p) -> Gamma(E_(p+1)),
d_(p+1) d_p = 0.
```

Every arrow decomposes on the same irreducible carrier:

```text
d_p = direct sum over pi of I_(V_pi) tensor d_(pi,p),

d_(pi,p): M_(pi,p) -> M_(pi,p+1),
d_(pi,p+1)d_(pi,p)=0.
```

The infinite-dimensional differential complex has therefore become, for each
representation label, a finite complex of multiplicity spaces.  This is the
retained human-executable object: branching determines its nodes and natural
intertwiners determine its arrows.

## De Rham specialization

Let `(G,K)` be a compact Riemannian symmetric pair with Cartan decomposition

```text
g = k direct-sum p.
```

At the base point, `p` is the tangent representation.  Complex differential
`p`-forms are sections of the homogeneous bundle generated by

```text
tau_p = exterior_power(p, p_C^vee),
Omega^p(G/K;C) ~= Gamma(G x_K tau_p).
```

The natural arrows are exterior differentiation and its Hilbert adjoint:

```text
d_p: Omega^p -> Omega^(p+1),
delta_p = d_(p-1)^*,
Delta_p = d_(p-1)delta_p + delta_(p+1)d_p.
```

For the metric induced by the negative Killing form, the compact-symmetric-space
Casimir theorem contract identifies the Hodge Laplacian with the negative group
Casimir.  If `c_pi>=0` is its scalar on `V_pi`, restriction to a multiplicity block
computes both realizations on the same input:

```text
d_(pi,p-1)d_(pi,p-1)^* + d_(pi,p)^*d_(pi,p)
  = c_pi I_(M_(pi,p)).
```

This finite Hodge identity is the generated spectral engine.  For `c_pi>0`, it
splits the block orthogonally into

```text
M_(pi,p)
  = image d_(pi,p-1) direct-sum image d_(pi,p)^*.
```

The zero block is cohomological and must be retained separately.  Metric scaling,
orientation, and middle-degree splitting remain explicit inputs.

## Common middle object: the equivariant spectral complex

For the de Rham family, package all degrees at a fixed representation label as

```text
C_pi^p = Hom_K(V_pi restricted to K, exterior_power(p,p_C^vee)),
C_pi^bullet = (... -> C_pi^p --d_(pi,p)--> C_pi^(p+1) -> ...).
```

The complete semantic decomposition is then

```text
(Omega^bullet(G/K),d,delta,Delta)
  ~= completed direct sum over pi of
     V_pi tensor (C_pi^bullet,d_pi,d_pi^*,Delta_pi).
```

This `C_pi^bullet` is the first concrete common object of the bilateral program.
The representation route constructs its nodes by restriction and branching; the
differential route constructs its arrows and harmonic quotients.  Equality of
these finite complexes, together with analysis-synthesis recovery, is stronger
than matching a spectrum or a named special function.

## Normalized arrow rather than component eigenforms

Let `v` be co-closed in a positive `c_pi` block.  Then

```text
delta d v
  = (Delta-d delta)v
  = c_pi v,

norm(dv)^2
  = <v,delta d v>
  = c_pi norm(v)^2.
```

Consequently

```text
U_(pi,p) = d_(pi,p)/sqrt(c_pi)
```

is a unitary map from the coexact source sector to the corresponding exact target
sector, with inverse `delta/sqrt(c_pi)`.  One Casimir value and one partial
isometry regenerate both sectors; explicit eigenforms are recovery data only when
the observable asks for them.

## Forward return type

```text
BundleSpectralCalculus = (
  isotropy types tau_p,
  admissible irreducibles pi,
  multiplicity spaces M_(pi,p),
  finite arrows d_(pi,p) and adjoints,
  Casimir or invariant-operator blocks D_(pi,p),
  harmonic/exact/coexact projectors,
  analysis and synthesis maps,
  observable recovery,
  branching, domain, normalization, and failure certificates
).
```

A missing branching rule returns `UnresolvedMultiplicity`, not an expansion of all
tensor components.  Failure of an invariant operator to preserve the declared
domain returns a domain obstruction even when its algebraic blocks exist.

## What the differential family reconstructs in reverse

The de Rham PDE family alone canonically reconstructs the graded operator algebra

```text
d^2=0,
delta^2=0,
{d,delta}=Delta,
[Delta,d]=[Delta,delta]=0.
```

Equivalently, with

```text
Q_1=d+delta,
Q_2=i(d-delta),
```

one has `Q_1^2=Q_2^2=Delta` and `{Q_1,Q_2}=0` on a common invariant core.  This is
a universal supersymmetric/factorization algebra of the Hodge system.  It does not
recover the isometry group: the same relations occur on every oriented Riemannian
manifold for which the analytic realization is defined.

Group reconstruction requires additional first-order arrows.  If contractions
`i_X` are retained, the Cartan operation constructs

```text
L_X = {d,i_X}.
```

The semantic checks

```text
[L_X,d]=0,
[L_X,i_Y]=i_[X,Y],
[L_X,L_Y]=L_[X,Y]
```

recover a represented Lie bracket when the symbol map `X -> i_X` is faithful.
Integration to a global group additionally requires completeness and topology;
kernels and covers remain ambiguity data.  From `Delta` alone, the inverse must
stop at the universal Hodge algebra or at a bounded commutant candidate.

## Bounded Cartan inverse constructor

Let a differential realization supply the graded algebra of forms and a finite
candidate family of degree-zero first-order operators `T_i`.  The inverse performs
semantic tests in this order:

1. **Derivation:** verify that `T_i` is a degree-zero derivation of the wedge
   product and that `[T_i,d]=0`.
2. **Symbol:** define the vector-field candidate on functions by
   `X_i(f)=([T_i,m_f] 1)`, where `m_f` is multiplication by `f`; reject any operator
   whose commutator is not multiplication by a derivation of functions.
3. **Cartan lift:** construct or verify a degree-minus-one contraction `i_i` with
   `T_i={d,i_i}` and the contraction Leibniz rule.
4. **Closure:** compute
   `[T_i,T_j]-sum_k c_ij^k T_k` on the common core.  Zero residual constructs a
   Lie-algebra representation; a nonzero residual is returned with the declared
   finite span.
5. **Effective quotient:** divide by the kernel of `X -> T_X` before naming the
   recovered algebra.
6. **Global integration:** require complete vector fields or the appropriate
   skew-adjoint generators, then retain all covers/quotients compatible with the
   action.

Discovery of the initial `T_i` is a separate bounded first-order commutant search.
The Cartan constructor certifies and organizes candidates; it does not claim to
enumerate every hidden symmetry of an arbitrary PDE.

## Bilateral relation

The correspondence is a span through a common calculus:

```text
RepresentationRealization ----> BundleSpectralCalculus
                                      <---- DifferentialRealization.
```

A bilateral witness consists of the two realization maps plus equality of the
finite blocks and recovery of the same observable.  It is not an assertion that
either realization uniquely determines the other.

## Human-computability certificate

For each admissible `pi`, retain:

- one branching computation for `M_(pi,p)`;
- one Casimir or invariant-operator value;
- the finite chain maps between multiplicity spaces;
- exact/coexact/harmonic projectors;
- one analysis-synthesis recovery map.

The residual problem size is `dim M_(pi,p)`, not the number of coordinate
components times the number of spatial modes.  Computational leverage is claimed
only after the cost of branching, block construction, and observable recovery is
compared with a coordinate baseline.

## Theorem contracts and boundary

- **Peter-Weyl/Frobenius reciprocity:** compact `G`, closed `K`, finite-dimensional
  unitary fiber type; supplies the discrete decomposition and finite multiplicity.
- **Casimir/Hodge identification:** compact symmetric pair with the specified
  invariant metric; supplies `Delta_p=-Omega_G` up to declared normalization.
- **Hodge decomposition:** compact oriented Riemannian manifold; supplies the
  orthogonal harmonic/exact/coexact decomposition for the closed realization.
- **Outside the horizon:** noncompact spaces use direct integrals and may contain
  continuous spectrum or discrete middle-degree terms; nonsymmetric homogeneous
  spaces need curvature and isotropy corrections rather than the bare Casimir.

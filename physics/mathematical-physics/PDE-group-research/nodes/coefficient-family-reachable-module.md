# Coefficient-family reachable module

## Frozen contract `C_G18_v1`

Let

```text
H(lambda)=sum_(a=1)^s f_a(lambda) H_a                         (1)
```

with a finite tuple of exact Hermitian matrices `H_a`, real-rational
specialization weights, one nonzero preparation `psi`, one Hermitian effect `Q`,
and finite resource bounds. G18 asks for one carrier that works for every
specialization, not a carrier inferred from selected sample points.

Variable preparations/effects, non-Hermitian dynamics, unbounded coefficient
operators, direct integrals, and PDE domain questions are outside this contract.

## Construction and proof object

Breadth-first closure applies each declared coefficient only to newly admitted
directions:

```text
K_0=span{psi},
K_(j+1)=K_j + sum_a H_a K_j,
K_family=stable value.                                        (2)
```

Exact Gaussian-rational elimination admits an image only when it is independent.
The algorithm stores action coordinates while discovering the basis, so it does
not enumerate words in the `H_a`. Finite ambient dimension makes (2) terminate.

For the resulting basis matrix `B`, it constructs

```text
H_a B = B A_a,       G=B^dagger B,       F=B^dagger Q B.       (3)
```

Every specialization then satisfies

```text
H(lambda)B = B A(lambda),   A(lambda)=sum_a f_a(lambda)A_a.    (4)
```

Thus the reduced exponential and pulled-back effect recover the same prepared
expectation. Each `A_a` is self-adjoint in the exact Gram metric:
`G A_a=A_a^dagger G`. As in G17, `Q` need not preserve the active carrier.

Minimality is constructive: every common invariant subspace containing `psi`
contains every stage of (2), hence contains `K_family`. In representation terms,
this is the cyclic module generated by `psi` under the represented associative
coefficient algebra. Lie closure can be computed on this module later; it is not
presupposed.

## G15 differential-family adapter

The finite-window Hamiltonian is derived before momentum samples as

```text
H(k,g)=g H_gap + k H_momentum + H_constant,
H_gap      =(I-2P)/2,
H_momentum =2 epsilon (P' arrow + adjoint),
H_constant =  epsilon (P'' arrow + adjoint).                  (5)
```

The adapter proves every already assembled G15 block equals the exact
specialization `(g,k,1)` of (5). It refuses varying carrier preparations or
effects rather than silently merging distinct cyclic modules.

## Executable certificate and cost

`construct_coefficient_family_active_subspace` returns `B`, all `A_a`, `G`, `F`,
closure checks, and separate exact-operation counts.
`specialize_active_hamiltonian` and `evaluate_family_expectation` reuse this
object. `construct_prepared_coefficient_family_active_window` preserves the G15
finite-window complement probability.

If the common active dimension is `r` and there are `s` coefficients, discovery
uses `sr` coefficient actions and `sr` exact coordinate solves. Pointwise G17
uses `sum_j r_j` Hamiltonian actions and solves. Neither expression includes the
shared reduced exponentials, and neither is collapsed into a fabricated scalar
cost.

## Evidence packet `E_G18_1`

The unchanged repeated-Pauli family was tested at multiplicities `m=2,3`, ambient
dimensions `q=4,6`, and window sizes `n=1,4`. The family has three generators.
In every case both the family and pointwise carriers have dimension three; every
block specializes exactly and complement-probability error is at most
`2.1e-17`.

Three alternating-order repetitions used CPython 3.14.6, NumPy 2.5.2, and SciPy
1.18.1 on Windows 11. G14 construction and exact block assembly were shared.

| `q` | `n` | family / pointwise exact solves | family / pointwise runtime |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 4 | 1 | `9 / 3 = 3.00` | `2.43` |
| 4 | 4 | `9 / 12 = 0.75` | `0.88` |
| 6 | 1 | `9 / 3 = 3.00` | `2.56` |
| 6 | 4 | `9 / 12 = 0.75` | `0.85` |

So one-point use rejects the leverage claim, while a four-point window removes
25% of exact discovery solves and was 12--15% faster on this environment. The
instrument is `computation/benchmark_active_subspace.py`; timings are local
observations, while closure and specialization equalities are exact tests.

An algebraic control has two Hermitian generators whose sum cancels the only
initial coupling. Pointwise closure at that sample has dimension one, whereas
the true family module has ambient dimension three. This proves that samplewise
agreement cannot certify family invariance and exercises the required no-gain
result.

## Disposition

- **Constructed exactly:** minimal common cyclic module, reduced generators,
  specialization intertwining, Gram self-adjointness, and effect pullback.
- **Supported transfer:** the G15/G17 family remains dimension three for two
  ambient multiplicities and all four momentum points.
- **Supported conditional leverage:** construction work crosses over between one
  and four points on this bench; no universal runtime claim follows.
- **Refused explicitly:** empty or non-Hermitian families, inexact/incorrect
  weights, exhausted budgets, and varying preparation/effect.
- **Negative control:** a common module can regrow to ambient dimension even when
  a sampled Hamiltonian has a tiny cyclic carrier.
- **Unresolved:** sparse scaling, conditioning, parameter-dependent
  preparations, unbounded coefficients, and PDE-domain lift.

The next bridge must therefore leave the sampled-block setting: lift (3) to an
actual matrix-valued differential operator `D=sum_alpha L_alpha tensor H_alpha`
and prove that coordinate changes of the scalar differential basis do not alter
the internal active module.

# Prepared-observable active-subspace propagation

## Frozen contract `C_G17_v1`

G17 begins after the local coefficient/projector construction has assembled a
finite exact Hermitian block. Its input is

```text
(H over Q(i), nonzero psi, Hermitian effect Q, finite time, resource bound). (1)
```

The requested output is the normalized expectation of `Q` after propagation.
No group label, eigenvector, special polynomial, or component catalogue is
supplied. Continuous spectra, unbounded operators, and original-PDE domain
control are outside this contract.

## Construction

Generate vectors only while the Hamiltonian produces a new direction:

```text
b_0=psi,  b_(j+1)=H b_j,
K_psi=span{b_0,...,b_(r-1)},
H b_(r-1)=sum_(j<r) c_j b_j.                                (2)
```

Exact Gaussian-rational elimination detects the first dependence. If `B` has
these vectors as columns, construct

```text
H B = B A,       G=B^dagger B,       F=B^dagger Q B.         (3)
```

The power basis is intentionally not orthonormalized: normalization could add
square roots outside `Q(i)`. Instead `G A=A^dagger G` retains self-adjointness in
the exact Gram metric. The reduced state starts at `e_0` and

```text
<exp(-itH)psi,Q exp(-itH)psi>
 = <exp(-itA)e_0,F exp(-itA)e_0>.                            (4)
```

Equation (4) does not require `Q K_psi` to lie in `K_psi`; `F` is the pullback of
the effect as a quadratic form. The tests include an effect that sends an active
basis vector outside the active carrier.

Closure proves invariance. Minimality is constructive: every `H`-invariant
subspace containing `psi` contains every vector in (2), hence contains
`K_psi`. The finite-dimensional exponential intertwining in (4) follows from
`H^n B=B A^n`; convergence of the matrix exponential is an admitted standard
theorem contract. Numerical evaluation is separated from these exact laws.

## Reusable tool and refusal

`construct_active_subspace` returns `B,A,G,F`, the closure coordinates, exact
checks, numerical effect recovery, a non-scalar cost ledger, and an honest
`no_dimension_gain`. `evaluate_active_expectation` reuses the constructed object
at another time. `construct_projector_active_window_propagation` composes G14
through G17 without first evaluating an ambient exponential.

The old real-rational Lanczos tool is unchanged. Complex Hermitian data has a
separate Gaussian-rational owner. Oversized carriers, insufficient active budget,
zero preparations, inexact data, non-Hermitian inputs, and numerical residuals
refuse distinctly.

For a human, the retained calculation is one relative polynomial relation (2),
one Gram matrix, and one pulled-back effect—not enumeration of eigenstates or a
catalogue of named polynomials. The relation is representation-theoretic: it
constructs the cyclic module generated by `psi` under the represented algebra
`Q(i)[H]`.

## Evidence packet `E_G17_1`

The unchanged non-rigid repeated-Pauli family from G16 was tested at multiplicity
`m=2,3`, ambient dimension `q=2m`, and window sizes `n=1,4`. Five
alternating-order repetitions used CPython 3.14.6, NumPy 2.5.2, and SciPy 1.18.1
on Windows 11. G14 projector construction and exact Hamiltonian assembly were
shared before timing.

| `q` | `n` | active dimensions | error | one-shot active/dense | 16-query ratio | 64-query ratio |
| ---: | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 4 | 1 | `3` | `4.34e-19` | `9.01` | `1.09` | `0.62` |
| 4 | 4 | `3,3,3,3` | `1.39e-17` | `11.93` | `1.50` | `0.68` |
| 6 | 1 | `3` | `4.34e-18` | `12.06` | `1.16` | `0.25` |
| 6 | 4 | `3,3,3,3` | `5.20e-18` | `59.38` | `1.57` | `0.50` |

The dense-exponential cubic proxy falls from `q^3` to `3^3` per block: totals
are `64 -> 27` and `216 -> 27` for one block. The active route additionally pays
three exact Hamiltonian actions, three exact coordinate solves, and eighteen
Gram/effect pairings per block. These exact and floating operations are not added
into a fabricated scalar proxy.

The runnable instrument is `computation/benchmark_active_subspace.py`. It retimes
the same assembled Hamiltonians over evenly spaced finite times, so the reuse
comparison changes neither dynamics nor observable.

## Disposition

- **Exact, finite support:** minimal invariant-carrier construction, Gram
  self-adjointness, effect pullback, and reusable-time recovery.
- **Transfer support:** every tested repeated-Pauli block reduces to dimension
  three, including ambient dimensions four and six and windows one and four.
- **Refuted on this bench:** one-shot runtime dominance; exact construction costs
  more than the tiny dense exponential.
- **Supported observationally:** 64-time reuse is faster in all four cases, by
  factors `1.46--4.04` in this environment.
- **Unresolved:** asymptotic crossover, sparse large carriers, conditioning of
  power bases, and family-uniform construction before specializing momentum.

G18 resolves the next bridge with a
[coefficient-family reachable module](coefficient-family-reachable-module.md):
one carrier is invariant under every Hamiltonian coefficient, specializes across
momentum, and removes repeated exact closure work after sufficient window reuse.

# Observable-cyclic spectral calculus

## 1. Capability and obstruction

A full group or eigenfunction decomposition often distinguishes states that the
preparation and observable never see. Conversely, beginning with a named family of
orthogonal polynomials presupposes the recurrence that the PDE should construct.

The requested capability is narrower and more general:

```text
(self-adjoint dynamics H, preparation psi, observable ell)
  -> smallest reducing carrier visible to that pair
  -> recurrence or spectral measure
  -> same correlator, resolvent, or survival amplitude.              (1)
```

This does not replace symmetry decomposition when the complete spectrum or many
sectors are needed. It constructs the minimal representation relative to one
capability.

Material bindings are the spectral-measure construction in
`../functional-and-spectral-methods.typ` and sources 30--31 in the
[source map](../sources.md#observable-cyclic-and-krylov-reduction).

## 2. The cyclic carrier is forced by spectral invariance

Let `H` be self-adjoint on a Hilbert space `Hcal`, with projection-valued spectral
measure `E_H`. Any closed reducing carrier containing `psi` must contain every
spectral projection `E_H(B)psi`. Therefore

```text
K_psi
 = closure span{E_H(B)psi : B a Borel set}
 = closure{f(H)psi : f bounded Borel}.                              (2)
```

is the smallest closed reducing carrier containing the preparation. For an
observable represented by a vector `phi`, no information outside it is needed
because

```text
<phi,f(H)psi> = <P_(K_psi)phi,f(H)psi>.                              (3)
```

Equation (3) evaluates both functionals on the same evolved state, which already
lies in `K_psi`; it is the observable-quotient witness.

For several preparations, replace (2) by the block-cyclic carrier generated by
their finite span. This retains polarization and spin blocks without expanding
each component into a separate scalar problem.

## 3. Spectral measure as the exact reduced representation

The preparation constructs the positive scalar measure

```text
mu_psi(B) = <psi,E_H(B)psi>.                                        (4)
```

The cyclic spectral theorem is used as a bounded theorem contract: for
self-adjoint `H`, the carrier (2) is unitarily equivalent to `L^2(mu_psi)` under a
map `A_psi` satisfying

```text
A_psi psi = 1,
A_psi H A_psi^(-1) = multiplication by lambda.                       (5)
```

Thus the survival amplitude and diagonal resolvent are not reconstructed from all
eigenvectors:

```text
<psi,exp(-itH)psi> = integral exp(-it lambda) dmu_psi(lambda),
<psi,(H-z)^(-1)psi> = integral dmu_psi(lambda)/(lambda-z).           (6)
```

For a block preparation, (4) becomes a positive matrix-valued measure and (5)
becomes a block multiplication representation. Degeneracy labels invisible to the
matrix measure are quotiented automatically.

## 4. The recurrence is generated, not catalogued

Suppose `psi` lies in the required power domains and polynomials are dense in
`L^2(mu_psi)`. Then the polynomial Krylov closure equals (2). The moments compute

```text
m_k=<psi,H^k psi>=integral lambda^k dmu_psi(lambda).                 (7)
```

Gram--Schmidt applied to `1,lambda,lambda^2,...` in `L^2(mu_psi)` constructs
orthonormal polynomials `p_j` and the three-term recurrence

```text
lambda p_j
 = beta_(j+1)p_(j+1)+alpha_j p_j+beta_j p_(j-1).                    (8)
```

Transporting (8) through (5) gives the same Lanczos recurrence directly on
`psi,Hpsi,...`. Its Jacobi operator `J` is the reduced realization of `H` on the
polynomial cyclic carrier, and on all of `K_psi` under the stated density
condition.

The direction of generation is therefore

```text
PDE/operator + preparation
  -> moments or Krylov arrows
  -> recurrence coefficients
  -> spectral measure and observable,
```

not “choose a special polynomial, then fit the PDE.” Hermite, Laguerre, Jacobi,
Krawtchouk, and other named families occur only when the generated coefficients
match their closed recurrence data.

## 5. Exact termination and controlled truncation

The exact finite case is decided by a relative minimal-polynomial certificate.
For the first `r` such that

```text
q_r(H)psi=0,          deg(q_r)=r,                                   (9)
```

the carrier has dimension `r`, `beta_r=0`, and the `r x r` Jacobi matrix gives
all functions of `H` visible to `psi` exactly. A symbolic zero or certified rank
test is required; a small floating-point coefficient is not (9).

Before exact termination, let `P_m` project onto the first `m` cyclic directions
and set `H_m=P_mHP_m`. The synthesis residual is

```text
R_m=(I-P_m)HP_m.                                                     (10)
```

In a Lanczos basis, (10) is the single boundary arrow proportional to the next
recurrence coefficient. The decision engine's Duhamel identity gives a finite-time
error whenever `R_m` is bounded on the declared sector. Sharper Gauss-quadrature,
resolvent, and analytic-function bounds may be imported only with their spectral
interval, moment, and finite-precision hypotheses.

This separates three statuses:

```text
beta_m=0 exactly                 -> ExactCyclicReduction,
certified observable error<=tol -> ControlledCyclicReduction,
uncertified early stop          -> FormalTruncation.
```

## 6. Relation to group representations

Suppose a group decomposition is already known:

```text
Hcal = direct integral/sum V_lambda tensor M_lambda.
```

The vector `psi` selects components in these sectors, and (4) pushes their
spectral weights into one scalar or block measure. Distinct irreducibles,
multiplicity vectors, or global groups that induce the same visible measure are
equivalent for (6). The cyclic calculus is therefore an observable quotient of a
group representation, not a competing claim about the full carrier.

In the inverse direction, the recurrence algebra of `J` is generally insufficient
to reconstruct a unique group. Extra tensor products, duals, intertwiners, and
global analytic data are needed. The machine may classify a closed recurrence or
ladder algebra, but it must retain group ambiguity.

## 7. Human and machine interface

The reusable constructor is

```text
CyclicReduce(H, Prep, Observable, zero_policy, tolerance, budget)
  -> carrier K,
     scalar/block recurrence J,
     analysis and synthesis maps,
     exact minimal-polynomial or truncation residual,
     observable recovery map,
     validity and ambiguity certificates.                            (11)
```

For a human, the retained object is one generative recurrence and one recovery
functional. For a machine, it is a matrix-free Krylov action with explicit zero
and error policies. Neither user must enumerate eigenfunctions, polynomial
coefficients, or irrelevant representation multiplicities.

### Exact finite executable

The contract now has a bounded implementation in
[computation/cyclic_reduce.py](../computation/cyclic_reduce.py), with its reproducible
interface and commands documented in the
[computation README](../computation/README.md). For a rational finite operator action
it constructs an unnormalized orthogonal recurrence

```text
q_(j+1)=Hq_j-alpha_j q_j-c_j q_(j-1),
c_j=<q_j,q_j>/<q_(j-1),q_(j-1)>.
```

Avoiding square roots keeps zero and termination tests exact. The output retains
`S(a)=sum_j a_jq_j`, `A(x)_j=<q_j,x>/<q_j,q_j>`, the tridiagonal reduced operator,
and the diagonal Gram metric. It checks `HS=SJ` at termination and
`J^*G=GJ`; at a budget stop it returns the one boundary residual instead. A
compact summary is the human object, while basis coordinates and checked moments
remain in the machine certificate.

This implementation is a finite exact backend for (11), not the full interface:
it supports one real rational preparation, an optional exact positive metric, and
the finite-time Duhamel bound. Block/spinor, complex, infinite, and
finite-precision spectral-measure cases remain open.

## 8. Boundary

- A non-self-adjoint operator needs biorthogonal or pseudospectral control; the
  positive measure (4) is then unavailable.
- A preparation outside required power domains may have a spectral measure but no
  usable moment recursion.
- Moment sequences can be ill-conditioned or indeterminate; the spectral theorem
  remains primary and the moment reconstruction requires its own certificate.
- Infinite `K_psi` is not a failure. It is useful only when the recurrence is
  generative or a truncation controls the named observable.
- Many preparations can make the block-cyclic carrier approach the full space.
- Constructing `Hpsi` may be as expensive as the original PDE solve; matrix-free
  notation alone does not establish leverage.

The exact finite use test is the
[coupled quadratic selector](../benchmarks/observable-cyclic-quadratic.md). The
[quartic finite-model transfer](../benchmarks/quartic-cyclic-transfer.md) now returns
exact no-gain and a controlled budget truncation in the parity block. A
continuum-PDE error bound remains the next analytic bridge.

# Polynomial lift Lie closure

## Tension and constructed object

G20 returns one exact local lift

```text
K_a=L_(v_a)-J_a,                    [D,K_a]=0,
```

plus constant internal ambiguity. One successful line is not yet a Lie algebra.
The next object is the smallest constant-real Lie algebra generated by several
exact lift pairs for the same polynomial operator and common core, followed by its
visible quotient. This is neither a group-name lookup nor function-field
involutivity.

### Frozen exploratory contract `C_G21_v0`

Input consists of one admitted polynomial `D`, finitely many pairs `(v_a,J_a)`,
their exact full-operator commutator witnesses, and a positive closure-dimension
budget. Every direction is a real polynomial vector field and every `J_a` is a
constant skew-Hermitian endomorphism. All pairs must act on the same coordinates,
carrier, operator, and common core.

Output is one of:

```text
LocalLiftAlgebra(g, z, g/z, structure_constants, provenance, cost)
SeedNotFullOperatorSymmetry(first_failed_identity)
UnresolvedClosureWithinBudget(generated_dimension, next_bracket, budget).
```

No finite-dimensionality, real-form name, integration, completeness of flow,
domain preservation, or global group is presumed.

## Closure operation

For pairs represented by `K=L_v-J`, direct operator commutation gives

```text
[(v,J),(w,H)] = ([v,w],-[J,H]),
[v,w]^k=sum_i(v^i partial_i w^k-w^i partial_i v^k).                         (1)
```

The sign in the internal component is forced by
`[L_v-J,L_w-H]=L_[v,w]+[J,H]`. Jacobi supplies the reusable certificate

```text
[D,[K_a,K_b]]=[[D,K_a],K_b]+[K_a,[D,K_b]]=0.                               (2)
```

Starting from an exact independent seed basis, evaluate previously unseen
brackets, add a bracket only when it increases the span over constant real
scalars, and stop at closure or before exceeding the budget. Polynomial
coefficients are lowered only as an exact execution representation. The human
object is the generated-subalgebra universal property, not the coefficient table.

Constant scalars are essential. Membership over `Frac(QQ[x])` would identify
`x^4 partial_x` with `x^2 partial_x`; that constructs a distribution or Lie
algebroid module, not a finite-dimensional Lie algebra with constant structure
coefficients.

## Principal-symbol finiteness gate

Open-ended bracket growth is avoidable for an important admitted class. If the
second-order principal part is

```text
D_2=-g^(ij) partial_i partial_j I_n
```

with constant nondegenerate real `g`, the order-two coefficient of `[D,L_v-J]`
is the flat Killing equation. After lowering an index,

```text
partial_i v_j+partial_j v_i=0.                                               (3)
```

Differentiate the three cyclic permutations of (3) and add/subtract them; every
second derivative of `v` vanishes. Hence every polynomial solution is

```text
v(x)=a+A x,                       A^T g+g A=0,                               (4)
```

in a space of dimension at most `d(d+1)/2`. Lower coefficients and the internal
action can only cut down this finite candidate space. Thus this symbol class should
construct its affine Killing module first and use one bounded defect kernel;
generic bracket saturation is only a fallback for symbols without such a
certificate.

## Internal ambiguity and visible quotient

Let

```text
S_D={(v,J):[D,L_v-J]=0},
Z_D={(0,J) in S_D}.
```

The projection `(v,J)->v` has kernel `Z_D`. For `(v,J) in S_D` and `(0,H) in
Z_D`, equation (1) has zero base part; equation (2) keeps it in `S_D`. Therefore
`Z_D` is an ideal and the visible quotient `S_D/Z_D` has a well-defined bracket.
An executable result nevertheless checks ideal membership, antisymmetry, and
Jacobi after exact coordinate lowering.

## Exact distinctions

For

```text
D=-Delta I_3+x x^T,                 x=(x,y,z)^T,                            (5)
```

two rotation pairs `(A_01 x,A_01)` and `(A_12 x,A_12)` generate the third pair.
Adding the scalar internal symmetry `(0,iI)` gives a four-dimensional exact local
algebra, a one-dimensional ambiguity ideal, and a three-dimensional visible
quotient with the `so(3)` structure table. The group label is recognized only
after the table is constructed.

Finite closure is not general even for a linear second-order PDE. For

```text
D=-partial_y^2,
K_2=x^2 partial_x,                  K_3=x^3 partial_x,                      (6)
```

both seeds commute with `D`, while

```text
[x^m partial_x,x^n partial_x]=(n-m)x^(m+n-1) partial_x.                    (7)
```

Equation (7) generates every degree `n>=2`; no finite-dimensional closure exists.
A bounded constructor must therefore return unresolved rather than infer a finite
Lie algebra. This exposes principal-symbol degeneracy as a possible source of
infinite symmetry growth, not merely an implementation cost.

## Disposition and horizon

Disposition: **frozen exploratory contract with an exact positive transfer, an
exact infinite-closure counterexample, and a constructed flat nondegenerate-symbol
finiteness gate**. The maintained checker constructs the quotient table for (5),
recovers all six affine Killing directions from a quadratic ansatz, refuses a
broken internal lift, and stops (6) at its stated budget. A reusable production
owner, G20-witness aggregation, variable-symbol finiteness, integration, and
boundary flow remain open.

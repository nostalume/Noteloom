# Residual direction and infinitesimal Lie lift

## Capability and new object

G19 returns an exact off-module differential operator

```text
R_B=sum_beta R_beta(x) partial^beta,       A_B R_B=0.                       (1)
```

The missing operation is not “recognize a rotation.” It is to decide whether the
positive-order leakage itself constructs a base derivation and, independently,
whether that derivation can be completed by an internal action to commute with the
original PDE. The intermediate object is a **projective derivation line**, not a
group name.

### Frozen contract `C_G20_v1`

Input:

- one exact G19 polynomial witness on a certified rank chart;
- its original polynomial operator `D=sum_alpha C_alpha partial^alpha`;
- the standard constant Hermitian carrier metric and compact-supported smooth
  common core;
- bounds on residual order, polynomial terms, rational-function elimination,
  internal dimension, and defect coordinates.

No group, coordinate symmetry, vector field, internal generator, Lie dimension,
or preferred matrix basis is supplied.

Output is one of:

```text
DirectionalLift(Lambda_R, primitive_v, ker(Phi), visible_lifts, ambiguity, cost)
NoDirectionalLeakage(nonzero_orders)
ResidualNotLineGenerated(rank, row_space_certificate)
HigherOrderResidualNotVectorField(first_higher_order)
NoInfinitesimalLift(Lambda_R, first_nonzero_defect)
UnresolvedWithinBudget(stage, required, available).
```

The successful result certifies only local differential-expression covariance.
It is neither Lie closure nor a global group action.

## Direction construction without component search

Suppose every residual coefficient of order greater than one vanishes. Flatten the
first-order symbol over
`K=Frac(QQ(i)[x_1,...,x_d])`:

```text
R_B^(1)=sum_i R_i partial_i,
A_R in Mat_(nr,d)(K),       A_R[(a,p),i]=(R_i)_(a,p).                       (2)
```

The exact row rank has a semantic interpretation:

- rank zero: the residual supplies no base direction;
- rank one: `Lambda_R=Row_K(A_R)` is a unique projective direction;
- rank greater than one: the residual is not generated by one common vector
  field, so v1 refuses instead of selecting an arbitrary row-space basis.

For rank one, take any nonzero polynomial row after the certified common
denominator is cleared, divide the multivariate polynomial gcd of its components,
and normalize one leading unit. This gives a primitive representative
`v=(v^1,...,v^d)` and derivation

```text
L_v=sum_i v^i partial_i.                                                    (3)
```

The normalization is certificate syntax. Multiplication by a nonconstant rational
function changes a vector field's bracket and flow, so the semantic output remains
the line together with the stated primitive chart representative.

### Naturality boundary

When higher-order leakage vanishes, a variable reduced-frame gauge `B -> B U(x)`
gives `R_i -> R_i U`; a constant unitary carrier change gives `R_i -> T R_i`.
Both act on `A_R` by invertible row operations over `K`, so `Lambda_R` is
unchanged. Under an invertible rational affine coordinate change `x'=Mx+b`,
`A_R -> A_R M^T`, exactly the component transformation of the derivation (3).

This is why the row space, rather than a chosen factor pair `N tensor v`, is the
retained object. Nonlinear chart changes requiring new localization certificates
are outside v1.

## Full-operator lift as one kernel

Fix a primitive representative and let `u(V)` be the constant skew-adjoint
endomorphisms of the carrier. Construct, rather than guess,

```text
Phi: k direct_sum u(V) -> Diff_<=m(V),
Phi(lambda,J)=[D, lambda L_v-J].                                            (4)
```

For constant `J`, the coefficient formula is finite:

```text
[D,L_v-J]
 = sum_(alpha,j) sum_(0<delta<=alpha)
     binom(alpha,delta) C_alpha (partial^delta v^j)
       partial^(alpha-delta+e_j)
   - sum_(alpha,j) v^j(partial_j C_alpha) partial^alpha
   + sum_alpha [J,C_alpha] partial^alpha.                                   (5)
```

Collecting polynomial coefficients turns (4) into one exact linear map. Let
`pi:ker(Phi)->k` be projection to the visible base coefficient.

- `im(pi)=0` means the residual line does not lift; pure internal centralizers are
  retained only as ambiguity evidence.
- `im(pi)!=0` gives a commuting pair `lambda L_v-J`.
- `ker(pi)` is the internal-centralizer ambiguity, so the inverse is not falsely
  reported as unique.

This is the bilateral composition sought by the worktable:

```text
failed active module -> residual line -> represented infinitesimal PDE symmetry
represented infinitesimal symmetry -> differential action -> G19 witness check.
```

The arrows meet at an operator identity, not at a name such as `SO(2)`.

## Why this is constructive and human-computable

For ambient rank `n`, active rank `r`, and base dimension `d`, direction discovery
uses the rank of one `(nr) x d` matrix over `K`; it does not enumerate polynomial
modes, special functions, coordinate separations, or candidate groups. The lift
uses `1+dim u(V)=1+n^2` intrinsic unknowns and one defect kernel. Matrix entries are
an execution substrate; the human object is the row-line universal property and
the kernel/visible-projection diagram.

This is a structural reduction, not yet a complete-route speedup. Exact
rational-function elimination can suffer polynomial expression swell, and the
canonical `u(V)` seed scales quadratically. For spinors or natural tensor bundles,
G1/G4 may replace `u(V)` by a smaller structure-generated internal seed without
changing (4). Such compression must be proved complete relative to its declared
structure before it replaces the canonical seed.

## Lie and analytic promotion ladder

A lifted pair must pass independent later gates:

1. combine independently constructed visible lines and certify constant-coefficient
   bracket closure;
2. quotient ineffective actions and identify a real form;
3. prove completeness/integrability of the base vector fields and exponentiate the
   internal representation;
4. prove preservation of measure, boundary conditions, and the chosen operator
   domain;
5. compose with the requested observable witness and evaluate complete-route cost.

Failure at a later gate does not erase the earlier local identity. Conversely, a
rank-two residual refusal does not assert that the PDE has no symmetry; it says
this one preparation-relative leakage does not canonically select one direction.

## Executable realization and disposition

The [benchmark](../benchmarks/residual-derived-infinitesimal-lift.md) checks four
frozen alternatives:

- rank-one leakage and a one-dimensional visible lift in the variable non-Pauli
  model;
- the same residual line but no lift after adding the scalar potential `xI`;
- rank-two leakage for the free Laplacian with preparation `(1,x,y)^T`;
- rank-zero first-order leakage for pure coefficient escape.

A variable reduced-frame gauge, constant carrier action, rational coordinate
action, common complex phase, and a direct mixed-order operator-composition law
also preserve the claimed identities. `polynomial_residual_lie.py` owns the pure
construction; the existing schema router exposes
`construct-local-infinitesimal-symmetry` without adding another handler. It uses
the shared exact defect compiler with `1+n^2` candidates and retains G19 on every
later refusal.

Disposition: **implemented, deductively and executablely supported local
contract**. Multi-line closure, global integration, domain flow, and
same-observable leverage are not implemented or promoted.

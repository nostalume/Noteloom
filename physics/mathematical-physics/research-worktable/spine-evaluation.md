# Spine evaluation

## Verdict

The feasible research object is a bounded reduction compiler, not a universal
PDE–group inverse:

```text
represented filtered operator algebra = candidate-generating middle language
ReductionWitness                    = capability-bearing result
```

A useful constructor returns analysis, synthesis, reduced dynamics, recovery,
residual/error, validity, and whole-route cost for a named observable—or an
explicit obstruction. A Lie group is integrated only when a candidate algebra
closes appropriately and global hypotheses are supplied.

## What the consolidation establishes

The earlier main risk was **formal-portfolio accumulation**: correct calculi did
not yet prove that they belonged to one machine. The common computation boundary
projects three heterogeneous direct routes into the same fields and outcomes while
retaining each full certificate under `detail`. The bounded quadratic router has
no backend name and changes its decision with preparation visibility. The Pauli
router now adds the missing bilateral test: independent representation-first and
matrix-PDE-first constructions meet at one bundle-valued channel witness.

This supports a limited integration claim:

- the shared witness is executable rather than prose only;
- exact, controlled, formal, obstructed, and unresolved results are distinguished;
- a route may be selected only relative to the same observable and accuracy;
- scientific backends retain their typed internal objects rather than being
  flattened into one artificial representation.
- failure of the rational full-mode probe need not prevent exact cyclic execution.
- a matrix fiber, grading, and Clifford ambiguity can survive the common interface;
  failure of the inverse spin-curvature lift does not erase the forward candidate.

It does not yet support automatic candidate generation across the full calculus
library. Backend-free discovery is supported only for
`quadratic-schrodinger/v1`, `pauli-landau-bilateral/v1`,
`pauli-landau-global/v1`, `su-adjoint-transition/v1`, `su-adjoint-pde/v1`, and
`matrix-pde-inverse/v1`, `matrix-symbol-covariance/v1`,
`matrix-adjoint-semigroup/v1`, and `natural-tensor-stabilizer/v1`, not as a
complete search engine. The `coupled-carrier-covariance/v1` schema extends that
bounded discovery boundary to exact metric/Hermitian links and their
principal-symbol projectors. The `coupled-operator-covariance/v1` continuation
applies the generated action to ordered curvature and lower-term defects before
optional observable promotion.

## Strongest evidence

- The rotated quadratic oscillator constructs equal factor and centralizer
  projectors, spectra, and heat traces, then selects factorization because it
  avoids the complete 20-column compatibility calculation.
- Observable-cyclic reduction returns exact invisible-sector removal, honest
  no-gain, controlled finite-time truncation, or a formal boundary from the same
  recurrence constructor.
- Boundary/orbit reduction progresses from operator-domain stabilizers to
  fixed-continuum-accuracy comparison, genuine 3D-to-2D quotient PDEs, and a
  warped domain where isotropy generates mode-dependent axis conditions.
- Polynomial closure and its independent parent-Lie construction produce the same
  finite interbasis observable while remaining Pareto-incomparable for different
  capabilities.
- The bilateral Pauli router constructs the same Clifford--Heisenberg channels
  from representation and PDE data, transfers across charge reversal, and refuses
  a bad spin-curvature lift. Its global continuation computes the Chern number,
  finite Heisenberg multiplicity, direct-integral measure, and heat trace density,
  while refusing nonintegral flux and a trivial scalar domain. The variable
  spin-texture transfer remains conditional.
- The adjoint transition router generates the two `SU(3)` octet intertwiners as
  bracket and traceless Jordan products. Ordered and exchange-reversed amplitudes
  separately recover both reduced couplings, rejecting either one-copy route;
  `SU(2)` removes the Jordan copy by Cayley--Hamilton.
- The adjoint PDE continuation constructs
  `T_Xu=a[q_X,(1+Delta)u]+b{q_X,(1+Delta)u}_0` on the compact `H^2 -> L^2`
  realization. The Casimir action closes one smooth matrix-coefficient copy and
  gives exact `A T_X S=R_X`; a weaker domain is refused.
- The PDE-first matrix closure takes four unlabeled rank-three coefficient seeds,
  grows them from dimension four to eight under exact commutators, certifies
  `sl_3(C)`, and reconstructs the same `(5,1)` exchange-paired amplitude. The
  rank-two transfer returns `sl_2(C)` and one channel; a proper nilpotent closure
  is refused instead of being promoted to the expected algebra.
- The symbol-covariance continuation generates all inner-derivation vector fields
  from those seeds, verifies the trace principal pairing, and derives eigenvalue
  three (or two) from its pairing-dual square. It reproduces the same visible
  amplitudes without a supplied covariance generator or eigenvalue and refuses a
  basis-dependent noninvariant principal pairing.
- The adjoint-semigroup continuation uses conjugate transpose and positivity to
  select `su(n)`, promotes the visible coefficient module through an explicit
  normalized-Haar/`H^2` theorem contract, and computes
  `O_Z(exp(-t Delta)q_Y)=tr(ZY)exp(-nt)`. It correctly retains `SU(n)/PSU(n)`
  ambiguity because the center is invisible, and refuses wrong real-form or
  domain data without invalidating the local algebraic witness.
- The natural-tensor route constructs metric-preserving candidates from
  `g^-1 Lambda^2(V*)`, compresses elasticity residuals to permutation-orbit
  coordinates, and exactly matches the raw stabilizer span. This changes the
  candidate-budget verdict and preserves the same plane-wave observable. The G2
  planner now generates this route from anonymous relation facts and refuses
  missing, ambiguous, or uncertified relations.
- The coupled-carrier route starts from `End(V) direct-sum u(S)`, imposes metric
  and link-covariance defects, discovers an ineffective fiber phase, and returns
  closed effective dimensions three and one in the rank-three/rank-two Clifford
  benches. It uses those relations to construct exact principal-symbol projectors.
- The full-operator continuation reuses the generated rank-three quotient as its
  candidate space. Constant curvature reduces it `3 -> 1`; Pauli zeroth-order and
  curvature-aligned scalar first-order terms preserve that generator. Curvature
  constructs the grading and axis, while exact square completion recovers the
  shifted longitudinal channels. Rotation transfers unchanged. Transverse scalar
  transport kills the action. Axial matrix transport preserves the action and now
  enters a separate two-sector coefficient-algebra constructor: restricted traces
  recover its roots, the minimal polynomial recovers its projectors, and the
  original longitudinal polynomial is synthesized sectorwise.
- The finite coefficient-algebra continuation removes the involution assumption.
  Exact Krylov dependence and bounded rational splitting refine two individually
  degenerate commuting coefficients into three joint idempotents, retain carrier
  multiplicity, and reconstruct multivariate polynomial operators. Rotation and
  generator order do not change the primitive projector set.

## Feasibility boundary

The program may claim:

- constructive reductions within declared algebraic/geometric grammars;
- computational leverage when whole-route same-observable cost is lower;
- human leverage when a compact generator replaces exhaustive sectors or special
  functions and retains reconstruction;
- explicit negative information when a candidate fails.

It may not claim:

- a useful group for every PDE;
- a bijective group–PDE correspondence;
- solvability from formal Lie or polynomial closure;
- generic nonlinear decomposition;
- universal route dominance or analytical completeness.

## Adjusted frontier

1. **Noncommutative representation-algebra recovery:** G8 closes the finite split
   commutative case. Construct the algebra, center, and commutant of noncommuting
   Hermitian generators; use primitive central idempotents and a
   double-centralizer certificate to distinguish irreducible type from repeated
   multiplicity without a supplied group or preferred block basis.
2. **Leverage adjudication:** retain the complete G3 selected-route break-even of
   9 separately from the stabilization-only value 8; measure runtime or prove a
   family-level symbolic bound before promoting broader computational leverage.
3. **Human-cost normalization:** compare generator size, semantic steps, required
   theorem inputs, and reconstruction depth across admitted routes.
4. **Analytic re-entry:** revisit the Pauli branch only for a boundary, variable
   curvature, noncompact transverse carrier, or explicit projector-kernel
   observable; more constant-field torus instances do not alter the verdict.

Further scalar axial domains, polynomial catalogues, arbitrary dimension, and
nonlinear systems are parked until they expose a failure of the common witness or
change the bounded capability verdict. The compact ownership and re-entry rules are
maintained in the [research graph](research-graph.md).

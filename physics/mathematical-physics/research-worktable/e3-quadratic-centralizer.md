# Complete rank-two centralizer on punctured `E^3`

## 1. Why this is a generality test

The earlier three-dimensional Coulomb route knew which rotation-covariant vector
type to try. That was humanly short, but it left the hardest inverse step—the
choice of candidate type—outside the machine. Here the metric, not Coulomb, fixes
the entire order-two search space first. Only then is the potential allowed to
select a kernel.

For constant-curvature metrics, rank-two Killing tensors are generated by
symmetric products of Killing vectors. On Euclidean three-space the six Killing
vectors are the translations `P_i` and rotations `R_i`. Their symmetric square
has 21 presentations, but the intrinsic identity

```text
P_x symmetric-product R_x
+ P_y symmetric-product R_y
+ P_z symmetric-product R_z = 0                         (1)
```

leaves a 20-dimensional module. The executable computes this rank and null
relation from tensor coefficients; it does not insert the number 20 or discard a
Coulomb-chosen direction.

## 2. Finite construction

On the punctured space introduce `s=x^2+y^2+z^2` and work in

```text
A_3=Q[x,y,z,s^(1/2),s^(-1/2)]/(s-x^2-y^2-z^2),          (2)
```

with the exact normal-form rule `x^2 -> s-y^2-z^2`. For
`H=-Delta/2+V`, every module tensor `K` is tested by the single linear map

```text
B_V(K)=d(K dV).                                         (3)
```

Each survivor is lifted by solving `dW=K dV` and constructing

```text
Q_K=-1/2 partial_i K^ij partial_j+W.                    (4)
```

Full differential-operator composition, including every Leibniz term, must then
give `[H,Q_K]=0`. This is a finite 20-column exact kernel problem, not a search
over unknown coefficient functions and not an enumeration of coordinate systems.

## 3. Coulomb selection and falsifier

For `V=-kappa/r`, the first-order map selects all three rotations. The complete
rank-two map has a ten-dimensional kernel:

```text
<H> + symmetric-square <R_x,R_y,R_z> + <A_x,A_y,A_z>.
                                                               (5)
```

Thus the three Runge--Lenz directions are output directions of (3). Their lower
terms `-kappa x_i/r` are reconstructed from `K dV`, not supplied. The independent
closure certificate names these three directions only after discovery, verifies
their membership in the returned kernel, and checks

```text
[J_i,J_j]=-epsilon_ijk J_k,
[J_i,A_j]=-epsilon_ijk A_k,
[A_i,A_j]=-2H epsilon_ijk J_k,
J dot A=A dot J=0,
A^2=2H(L^2+1)+kappa^2                              (6)
```

in units `mu=hbar=1`. On a negative-energy shell these relations construct
`so(4)=su(2)+su(2)` and its equal-spin condition, hence

```text
E_n=-kappa^2/(2n^2),             multiplicity=n^2.       (7)
```

Changing only the input to `V=-kappa/r+r^2` leaves the three rotations and the
seven-dimensional quadratic sector `<H>+Sym^2<R_i>`, while all three Runge--Lenz
directions disappear. This separates symmetry forced by radiality from symmetry
forced by the inverse-radius coefficient.

## 4. Computability and boundary

For a human, the retained proof is the quotient (1), the kernel map (3), the
decomposition (5), and the closure/Casimir (6). Tensor components live in the
machine certificate only as replay evidence. For a computer, exact finite linear
algebra replaces differential determining equations; the candidate count is fixed
by geometry before the potential is read.

The result is complete only for scalar, time-reversal-even differential
centralizers of order at most two within the declared radial coefficient algebra.
The finite inverse-derivative carrier used for `W` may refuse outside the tested
forms. The puncture, self-adjoint realization, strong commutation, existence and
completeness of bound eigenspaces, higher-order integrals, magnetic/Clifford
modules, and nonlinear Lax or recursion structures remain separate obligations.

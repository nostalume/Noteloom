# Variable-projector differential jet

## Frozen contract `C_G11_v1`

G10 constructs an embedding `B` of one irreducible carrier when its projector is
constant. For a moving embedding, differentiation itself creates the obstruction:

```text
partial(B phi)=B partial(phi)+(partial B)phi,
partial^2(B phi)=B partial^2(phi)+2(partial B)partial(phi)
                     +(partial^2 B)phi.                              (1)
```

Pointwise algebra decomposition is therefore not PDE decomposition unless the
additional terms are constructed and either retained or proved zero.

## Exact transported jet

The admitted local family is generated by an exact skew-Hermitian matrix `R`:

```text
B'=RB,       B''=R^2B,       P'=[R,P].                               (2)
```

This is a first/second jet; no global exponential or coordinate eigenvectors are
needed. Skew-Hermiticity makes the transport infinitesimally unitary. The machine
checks the differentiated projector law

```text
P'=P'P+PP'.                                                           (3)
```

It then decomposes every frame derivative through the G10 analysis/synthesis map:

```text
B'=B Gamma+K,       PK=0,
B''=B Phi+L,        PL=0.                                             (4)
```

`Gamma` is the local connection matrix in the constructed frame. `K` is the
first off-block or second-fundamental-form arrow; `Phi,L` retain the second-order
terms. For amplitude jets `(phi,phi',phi'')`, substitution of (4) into (1) gives

```text
partial^2(B phi)
 = B(phi''+2 Gamma phi'+Phi phi) + (2K phi'+L phi).                    (5)
```

The implementation evaluates both sides over `Q(i)`. Exact decoupling is returned
only when every column of `K` and `L` vanishes.

## Frame covariance

For a constant invertible reduced frame change `B_new=B G`, the constructor
re-solves all intertwiner coordinates in the new frame. The reduced matrices and
connection coordinates may change, while synthesized carrier vectors in (5) must
not. G11 tests this common endpoint rather than comparing coordinate entries.

## Evidence

With `R=0`, `P'=K=L=0`; G10 is recovered exactly. For the repeated-Pauli carrier,
the rational skew generator rotating coordinates zero and one moves the constructed
rank-two primitive projector. It yields `P'!=0`, a nonzero first leakage column,
and `exact_decoupling=false`. A section derivative along that column produces
nonzero leakage, while (5) reconstructs the direct second derivative exactly.

Swapping the two reduced frame vectors changes the local representation but leaves
the carrier-level direct derivative and synthesis unchanged. A Hermitian transport
generator returns `NonSkewTransportGenerator`; a wrong-sized gauge returns
`GaugeDimensionMismatch`.

## Complexity and boundary

For carrier size `q` and retained size `d`, dense construction uses a constant
number of `q x q` products plus `d` exact coordinate solves, conservatively
`O(q^3+d^2q^2)` arithmetic and `O(q^2+qd)` stored scalars. This is a local exact
jet cost, not a global PDE solve.

Disposition: **supported bounded** for an exact Gaussian-rational transported jet
of a G10 carrier. G11 constructs the connection and leakage terms that pointwise
representation decomposition misses and preserves the constant-projector limit.

It does not infer a spectral gap, operator norm, domain invariance, propagation
time, or small parameter. Consequently nonzero leakage is diagnosed, not called
adiabatically small. G12 should add a declared gap and observable window, compute a
leakage bound, and compare the controlled reduced evolution with the full one under
the theorem contracts already recorded in the mode-bundle calculus.

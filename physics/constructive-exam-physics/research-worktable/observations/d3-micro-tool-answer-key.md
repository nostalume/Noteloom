## Product answers

With phasors `a=A` and `b=B(c+is)`,

```text
mean(xy)=ABc/2,
ripple phasor=AB(c+is)/2.
```

| Variant | mean | `Re(ripple)` | `Im(ripple)` |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| D3-A-H1 | 3 | 3 | 4 |
| D3-A-H2 | 12 | 12 | 9 |
| D3-I-H1 | 50/13 | 50/13 | 120/13 |
| D3-I-H2 | 180/13 | 180/13 | 75/13 |
| D3-D-H1 | -3 | -3 | 4 |
| D3-D-H2 | 28/5 | 28/5 | 96/5 |

The real ripple is

```text
Re(ripple exp(2i omega t))
  = Re(ripple) cos(2omega t) - Im(ripple) sin(2omega t).
```

## Scoring boundary

Correctness requires every requested target plus its unit/sign/regime return. Score
route adherence independently. Do not mark a mathematically correct direct solution
as operator-adherent, and do not convert an incorrect fast attempt into a timing
observation.

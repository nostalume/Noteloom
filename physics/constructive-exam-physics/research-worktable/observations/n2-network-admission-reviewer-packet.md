# N2 independent admission-key reviewer packet

Use the three decision definitions in the training sheet, but do not consult the
administrator key or another reviewer. For each task:

1. reconstruct the reciprocal/symmetry and scalar-pivot checks;
2. when admitted, compare declared and minimum-fill action ledgers under strictly
   positive but unspecified action weights;
3. select `direct_gate`, `conditional_candidate`, or `refuse`;
4. record the exact inequality or obstruction supporting that selection;
5. judge whether the participant prompt determines the intended structural class
   without requiring hidden conventions.

Submit the independent classifications before the administrator reveals the key.
Then record agreement and ambiguity judgments in the review TSV. A false judgment
requires an explanation in `notes`. Record real expertise and provenance; an
unidentified, unblinded, or non-independent review cannot count toward readiness.

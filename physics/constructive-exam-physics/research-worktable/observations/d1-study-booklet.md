# D1 matched-route study booklet

Do not open the separate answer key until both blocks are complete and recorded.
This packet contains authored numerical variants of six A617 problem structures;
they are not additional official examination questions.

## Conditions and timing

- Use paper only: no notes, formula sheet, CAS, or solution lookup.
- Assign each participant exactly one sequence, `S1` or `S2`.
- Complete block 1, then complete block 2 after a declared interval; one day is
  preferred. Do not carry working between blocks.
- Start timing when the problem and assigned route are revealed.
- Stop after the numerical or exact answer, unit, and required branch/regime check
  have been written.
- Retain incorrect and unfinished work. Do not convert it into a shorter time.

## Route cards

**Direct route:** use the shortest competent physical-law dependency chain. Do not
encode the complete law packet for simultaneous elimination.

**Proposed route:** write `GIVEN | HIDDEN | TARGET`, form the target-connected
relation packet, eliminate hidden quantities by atomic subjects, and finish with the
physical return ledger.

## Problems

### A25-03-A

An electron of total energy 5 MeV crosses a region `11/60` light-years wide. Its
rest energy is 0.5 MeV. Using 360 days per year, find the elapsed proper time in
days.

### A25-03-B

An electron of total energy 8.5 MeV crosses a region `2/5` light-years wide. Its
rest energy is 0.5 MeV. Using 360 days per year, find the elapsed proper time in
days.

### A24-09-3-A

An air-filled parallel-plate capacitor has area `0.020 m^2` and separation
`0.004 m`. A metal slab of thickness `0.001 m` fills the plate area without
touching either plate. The capacitor is charged to `300 V`, disconnected, and the
slab is removed quasistatically. Find the required external work in joules.

### A24-09-3-B

An air-filled parallel-plate capacitor has area `0.030 m^2` and separation
`0.006 m`. A metal slab of thickness `0.002 m` fills the plate area without
touching either plate. The capacitor is charged to `200 V`, disconnected, and the
slab is removed quasistatically. Find the required external work in joules.

### A22-03-1-A

A particle of mass `1 kg` moves at `v=3c/5`. A force of `125 N` is applied parallel
to its velocity. Find its laboratory acceleration in `m/s^2`.

### A22-03-1-B

A particle of mass `2 kg` moves at `v=4c/5`. A force of `250 N` is applied parallel
to its velocity. Find its laboratory acceleration in `m/s^2`.

### A15-08-A

Thermal neutrons are in equilibrium at `300 K` and have mean kinetic energy
`3 k_B T/2`. Find their de Broglie wavelength in metres using neutron mass `m_n`
and Planck constant `h`.

### A15-08-B

Thermal neutrons are in equilibrium at `675 K` and have mean kinetic energy
`3 k_B T/2`. Find their de Broglie wavelength in metres using neutron mass `m_n`
and Planck constant `h`.

### A13-02-A

A vertical water jet leaves an opening of area `0.001 m^2` at `10 m/s`, has density
`1000 kg/m^3`, and supports an inverted cylinder of mass `9 kg`. Half the water
sticks and comes nearly to rest; the rest rebounds vertically downward at the
impact speed. Take `g=10 m/s^2` and neglect drag and spreading. Find the stationary
height above the opening.

### A13-02-B

A vertical water jet leaves an opening of area `0.001 m^2` at `8 m/s`, has density
`1000 kg/m^3`, and supports an inverted cylinder of mass `6 kg`. Half the water
sticks and comes nearly to rest; the rest rebounds vertically downward at the
impact speed. Take `g=10 m/s^2` and neglect drag and spreading. Find the stationary
height above the opening.

### A11-03-A

A spaceship of proper length `150 m` moves at `v=4c/5`. When its tail passes a
ground station, the station emits a forward light pulse. In the ground frame, find
the ship-head distance from the station and the elapsed time when the pulse reaches
the head. Use `c=3.00e8 m/s`.

### A11-03-B

A spaceship of proper length `125 m` moves at `v=3c/5`. When its tail passes a
ground station, the station emits a forward light pulse. In the ground frame, find
the ship-head distance from the station and the elapsed time when the pulse reaches
the head. Use `c=3.00e8 m/s`.

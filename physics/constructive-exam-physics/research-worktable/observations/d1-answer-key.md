## A25-03 proper time

Use `gamma=E/E_rest`, `gamma beta=sqrt(gamma^2-1)`, and

```text
proper days = 360 distance_in_light_years / (gamma beta).
```

- A25-03-A: `gamma=10`, so `tau=2 sqrt(11) days`.
- A25-03-B: `gamma=17`, so `tau=6 sqrt(2) days`.

Require the positive future-directed speed and positive proper-time branch.

## A24-09-3 capacitor work

With `A=epsilon_0 S`,

```text
C_m=A/(d-l),  C_0=A/d,  Q=C_m U_0,
W=Q^2/(2C_0)-Q^2/(2C_m)
 = epsilon_0 S U_0^2 l/[2(d-l)^2].
```

- A24-09-3-A: `W=100000 epsilon_0 J`.
- A24-09-3-B: `W=75000 epsilon_0 J`.

Require fixed charge after disconnection, `0<l<d`, and positive work.

## A15-08 neutron wavelength

From `p^2/(2m_n)=3k_B T/2` and `lambda p=h`,

```text
lambda = h/sqrt(3 m_n k_B T).
```

- A15-08-A: `lambda=h/[30 sqrt(m_n k_B)]`.
- A15-08-B: `lambda=h/[45 sqrt(m_n k_B)]`.

The B wavelength is `2/3` of the A wavelength. Require positive momentum and
wavelength.

## A13-02 water-jet height

The mass flux is `rho S v_0`. If the impact speed is `v`, the upward momentum-flux
force is

```text
(2-alpha) rho S v_0 v = mg,
v^2 = v_0^2-2gH.
```

- A13-02-A: `v=6 m/s`, `H=16/5 m`.
- A13-02-B: `v=5 m/s`, `H=39/20 m`.

Require `v>=0`, `H>=0`, and the downward rebound convention.

- A11-03-B: `L=100 m`, meeting distance `250 m`, meeting time `5/6 microseconds`.

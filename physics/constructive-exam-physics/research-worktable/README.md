# Constructive Exam Physics Research Worktable

This directory owns inspectable research state. The
[active research graph](research-graph.md) is the sole mutable account of current
claims, evidence gaps, and the next valid frontier. Other documents supply
contracts, derivations, data, or historical results; they do not independently
declare the project current.

## Construction spine

- [framework](framework.md) defines the admissible-state relation, separates
  area-specific physics from reusable operations, reconstructs the physical
  return, and preserves the direct/refusal boundary.
- [benchmark](benchmark.md) freezes the active case contract and evidence cutoff.
- [computation](computation/README.md) owns setup, direct module imports, tests,
  commands, and executable limitations.

## Evidence collections

- [materials](materials.md) records provenance and source limitations.
- [A617 source audit](sources/a617-local-corpus.md) and
  [machine index](sources/a617-corpus-index.tsv) bind the local corpus.
- [results](results/) contains immutable result cutoffs and construction reports.
- [observations](observations/) contains study protocols, booklets, keys, and empty
  acquisition templates.

Result filenames are historical evidence owners. Their counts and claims remain at
their own cutoffs; the active graph determines how they contribute to the current
disposition.

## Evidence discipline

- A mathematical certificate does not establish source interpretation.
- A source-bound regression does not establish unseen transfer.
- A smaller symbolic graph does not establish lower human time or error.
- Empty review or learner templates mean `unobserved`, not zero effect.
- No document may promote universal coverage, learner benefit, or novelty without
  a named evidence owner in the active graph.
